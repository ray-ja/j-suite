Findings requiring your attention
Finding #1 · Minor
Observed
The outdoor shower is draining noticeably slower than on the last two visits — water standing roughly 2″ deep and taking about a minute to clear. Most likely sand and hair in the trap; no sign of a leak or a broken line.
Action taken
Pulled and cleared the visible debris at the grate, then ran it again to confirm. It improved but did not clear fully. I did not open the trap — that's plumbing work and outside what I do on a watch visit.
Recommendation
Have the trap pulled and cleared before the season. I can meet a plumber and let them in on any weekday, or handle it on my next visit for the standard hourly rate if you'd rather I try first. No urgency — it drains, just slowly.
