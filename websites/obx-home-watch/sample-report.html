Noted for your attention
Item #1 · Outdoor shower drain
Observed
The outdoor shower drained noticeably slower than on the last two visits — water standing roughly 2″ deep and taking about a minute to clear. Sand and hair visible at the grate; no water seen anywhere it shouldn't be.
What we did
Pulled and cleared the visible debris at the grate, then ran the shower again. It improved but did not clear fully. We did not open the trap — that's plumbing work, outside what we do on a watch visit.
Suggested next step
Have the trap pulled and cleared before the season. We can meet a plumber and let them in on any weekday, or have a go at it ourselves on the next visit at the standard hourly rate if you'd rather. No urgency — it drains, just slowly.
