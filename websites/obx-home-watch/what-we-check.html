Inside

The interior walk-through

Interior checklist

  • Every room entered and eyeballed — ceilings, walls, floors
  • Temperature and humidity logged (the mold early-warning)
  • HVAC running correctly; filter condition noted
  • Under every sink — supply lines, traps, cabinet base for damp
  • Water heater base and pan checked for weeping
  • Toilets checked; traps refilled to block sewer gas
  • Faucets/showers run briefly to keep traps wet and lines fresh
  • Refrigerator/freezer operating; ice-maker line checked
  • Signs of pests, rodents or insect activity
  • Windows and doors secure; no evidence of entry
  • Smoke/CO alarm chirps noted; breaker panel scanned
  • Any odour, stain or change from last visit — photographed
Outside

The exterior pass

Exterior checklist

  • Roofline and visible shingles from ground level
  • Siding, trim and soffits — storm or salt damage
  • Decks, stairs, railings and pilings
  • Windows and sliders — seals, glass, screens
  • Gutters and downspouts flowing, not overflowing
  • Crawlspace vents and access secure and dry
  • Outdoor shower, spigots and hose bibs
  • HVAC condenser clear and undamaged
  • Yard, dune line and drainage after heavy rain
  • Mail, packages and flyers cleared (an empty-house tell)
  • Vehicles, boats or trailers on the property still where they should be
  • Anything that shouldn't be there — photographed

Every home is different. On the first walk-through we note yours specifically — the shut-off that sticks, the window that leaks in a nor'easter — and those go on your checklist permanently.
