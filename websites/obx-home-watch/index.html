Why owners call us

Little problems stay little when someone's watching.

Salt air, humidity and storms work on a coastal house year-round. We catch the small stuff (a drip, a tripped HVAC, a torn screen) while it's still a quick, cheap fix, and you hear about it the same day.

Water damage

A water line starts dripping in January and nobody notices until spring. It's the single most expensive thing that happens to a vacant home.

Humidity & mold

HVAC breaker trips off, humidity climbs, and mold moves in. We log temperature and humidity every visit to prevent it,

Storm prep & damage checks

On request, we prep and photograph before the storm and get eyes on the property after it, so you know within a day. Storm service →

Freeze & cold snaps

Every visit we confirm the heat is set appropriately and log the reading. When a hard freeze is coming, we'll go set your faucets to drip on request to prevent bursts.

Pests & rodents

Mice, ants, and worse move into quiet houses. We look in the spots they love to catch them early.

Insurance exposure

Many policies limit or void coverage on a home left vacant and unchecked. Documented visits show the house was looked after, in writing, with dates.

The insurance angle

Your policy probably treats an empty house differently.

Standard homeowner policies commonly suspend some coverage once a dwelling has been vacant past 30 or 60 days, and freeze-related water damage is often excluded unless heat was maintained or the water was shut off. The exact wording varies by carrier, so ask your agent what your policy says. What we can do is give you the evidence: a dated, photographed record from every visit showing that someone verified the heat, the water and the condition of the house. It's archived and ready to hand to an adjuster.
