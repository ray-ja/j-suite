What Is Home Watch — and Do OBX Owners Need It?

Home watch is a scheduled check on a property you do not live in. We walk the home inside and out, look for leaks, pests, storm and break-in damage, run water, and send you a photo report after every visit.

Because most OBX homes sit empty for much of the year — and many insurers require periodic checks on vacant homes — home watch catches small problems before they become expensive disasters.

  • Interior + exterior inspection
  • Photo report every visit
  • Pre- and post-storm checks
