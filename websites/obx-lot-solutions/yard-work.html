Routine outdoor work

Mow, trim, edge — and haul off the mess

Keep your property looking cared-for between visits. We handle the routine outdoor work — mowing, trimming, edging — and clear the leaves, brush, and storm debris that pile up out here. One-time when you need it, or on a recurring schedule so you never think about it.

Yard work pairs naturally with home-watch and washing — we already know the property, so one visit can keep the whole exterior in shape. Recurring is the goal: it's sticky, simple, and saves you 20% per visit.

What we don't do (on purpose)

We cut limbs and branches, clear brush, and take down small trees we can safely reach from the ground or a ladder — we don't climb trees or remove large ones. Anything beyond that we refer out to a licensed, insured tree service. It keeps everyone safe and your property protected.
