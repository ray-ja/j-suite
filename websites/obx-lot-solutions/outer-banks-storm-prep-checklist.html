Outer Banks Storm-Prep Checklist for Absentee Owners

Before the storm: secure or store outdoor furniture and grills, clear gutters and check drainage, photograph the property for insurance, and confirm your home-watch contact has access.

After the storm: a fast damage check and photo report so you can file insurance early if needed, plus board-up if there is any breach.

  • Pre-storm: secure, clear, photograph
  • During hurricane season: keep a local on call
  • Post-storm: fast check + photo report
