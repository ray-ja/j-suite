The full checklist

Here it is — no gate

We believe in giving value up front. Read or print it now.

Exterior walk

  • Roof & gutters — debris, sagging, storm damage
  • Siding & trim — algae, loose boards, wasp/insect nests
  • Windows & doors — seals, cracked panes, forced-entry signs
  • Decks, rails & stairs — rot, loose boards, wobble
  • Foundation & crawlspace — standing water, vents, pests
  • HVAC condenser, propane tank, outdoor outlets

Interior walk

  • Under every sink & the water heater — leaks or stains
  • Ceilings & walls — water marks, mold smell
  • Run faucets, flush toilets, check for slow drains
  • HVAC running; set thermostat for the season
  • Refrigerator/freezer cold; no spoiled-food odor
  • Breaker panel — nothing tripped; smoke/CO detectors
  • Pests — droppings, ants, signs of mice

Security & storm

  • All doors/windows locked; locks working
  • Exterior lights / timers working
  • Nothing disturbed; no signs of entry
  • Before a storm: secure furniture, clear drains, photos
  • After a storm: fast damage check + photo report

Every visit

  • Take date-stamped photos
  • Send the owner a short report
  • Note anything that needs follow-up
