Common questions

Do you provide the truck?

We provide the labor — the crew that loads, unloads, and carries. You bring or rent the truck, trailer, or POD. If you also need it hauled away, our junk-removal service covers the truck and disposal.

How is moving help priced?

By the hour, with a one-hour minimum. A two-person crew starts at $120/hour; single-worker help is $75/hour. We give a free estimate of the hours up front.

Can you move heavy or awkward items?

Yes — furniture, appliances, and awkward pieces. We use straps and moving blankets to protect your floors, walls, and belongings. Specialty items like pianos or gun safes we'll refer to a specialist.
