Tear-down + haul-off

Clear the lot — no leftover pile

We demolish sheds, small outbuildings, decks, and play structures, then haul the debris off and dispose of it the right way — you're not left with a pile to deal with. We protect what's staying, sweep up after, and send photos start to finish.

Price depends on the footprint and a few things that make a tear-down harder — roofing, a wood floor, how it's anchored, and how close the truck can get. We'll give you an honest written number on site, usually within about 15% once we've walked it.

A few things to know first

No concrete-slab removal. Slabs and foundations are a separate quote — breaking and hauling concrete is heavy work. By default the slab stays and we take the structure off it.

Power off first. Any wired power, lights, or outlets must be disconnected at the source by you or an electrician before we touch it.

Contents cleared first. We demolish an empty structure. If it's full, we'll add a junk haul for the load — easy to bundle.

FAQ

Common questions

What size structures do you demolish?

Sheds, small outbuildings, decks, and play structures. Anything larger or with a permitted foundation we quote on site and refer out when it needs a licensed demolition contractor.

Do you remove the concrete slab?

Slab and foundation removal is a separate quote — breaking and hauling concrete is heavy work. By default the slab stays and we remove the structure on top of it.

Do I need to disconnect power first?

Yes. Any wired power, lights, or outlets must be disconnected at the source by the owner or an electrician before we start, for everyone's safety.

What if the shed is full of stuff?

We demolish an empty structure, but we can clear it out first — we'll add a junk-removal line for the contents and handle it all in one visit.
