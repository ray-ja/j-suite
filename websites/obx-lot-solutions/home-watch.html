FAQ

Common questions

What is home watch?

A scheduled check on a property you do not live in. We inspect inside and out for leaks, pests, storm and break-in damage, run water, and send a photo report every visit.

How often do home-watch visits happen?

We offer monthly, bi-weekly, and weekly plans, plus one-time and pre/post-storm checks. We recommend a cadence based on your home and insurance.

What do you check on each visit?

Leaks and water issues, storm and wind damage, pests and signs of intrusion, HVAC and breakers, doors/windows/locks, and packages or mail.

Do I get a report after each visit?

Yes — a photo report by text or email every visit, so you have proof everything is fine or a fast heads-up if it is not.

Does my insurance require home watch?

Many insurers require periodic checks on vacant or seasonal homes. Our documented visits help you stay compliant and catch problems early.
