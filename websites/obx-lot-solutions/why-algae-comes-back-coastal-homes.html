Why Algae Keeps Coming Back on Coastal Homes

That film is living algae and mildew thriving on salt, humidity, and shade. Pressure alone knocks it off the surface but leaves the roots behind, so it returns fast — often within months.

Soft washing applies a cleaning solution that kills the growth at the root, which is why a proper soft wash stays clean far longer than a quick blast. On the coast, an annual soft wash is the realistic maintenance rhythm.

  • Algae feeds on salt + humidity + shade
  • Pressure only removes the surface
  • Soft wash kills the root → lasts longer
