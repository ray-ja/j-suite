What areas do you serve?

We serve the northern Outer Banks: Corolla, Duck, Southern Shores, Kitty Hawk, Kill Devil Hills, Nags Head, Manteo, and Lower Currituck.

Are you insured?

Yes — we carry $1M general liability insurance, so you're covered while we work on your property.

What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution for roofs, siding, and screens. Pressure washing uses high-pressure water for concrete. We use the right one for each surface. More on washing →

Will pressure washing damage my roof or siding?

We never pressure-wash roofs or siding — it strips shingles and forces water behind siding. Those get a safe soft wash that lasts longer.

How often should I wash my house here?

About once a year for most coastal homes — salt and humidity grow algae fast. A yearly recurring plan keeps it clean and saves 20%.

How much does a house wash cost?

House soft washes start around $349 (more for two-story), driveways $89–$189, roof soft washes from $449. Free written quotes. Get a quote →

What is home watch?

Scheduled checks on a home you don't live in — inside and out for leaks, pests, storm and break-in damage — with a photo report every visit. Home watch details →

How often do home-watch visits happen?

Monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly, plus one-time and pre/post-storm checks. We'll recommend a cadence for your home and insurance.

Do I get a report after each visit?

Yes — a photo report by text or email every visit.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes — recurring washing, cleanup, and turnover services across multiple properties.
