Our story

Reliability isn't a slogan for us — it's a habit.

Our founder came up as an Army medic, where showing up on time and doing the job right wasn't optional — people counted on it. That discipline is the whole company. When we say we'll be at your property Tuesday, we're at your property Tuesday.

We're a small, family-run crew rooted right here on the Outer Banks. We grew up around these beaches, we know what salt air and storms do to a home, and we treat your property like it's our own — because our neighbors' homes basically are.

Most homes out here belong to people who live somewhere else. That's exactly who we're built for: owners who need a trustworthy local crew to wash, watch, and care for a place they can't always get to. You get photos, honest communication, and work that's actually done.
