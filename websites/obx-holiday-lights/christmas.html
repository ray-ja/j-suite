What we light

Rooflines & peaks

The part you should not be doing yourself. Gutter line, peaks, dormers and gables — clipped, never stapled, and never drilled.

Trees & landscape

Live oaks, palms, crepe myrtles and foundation shrubs. Trunk wraps, canopy lighting and mini-light washes.

Columns & railings

Porch posts, deck railings, stair rails and the long ground-level runs that make a beach house read at night.

Docks & walkways

Soundside docks, boardwalks and dune crossings. Weather-rated fittings, because everything here lives in salt.

Wreaths & garland

Doors, windows and railings, lit or unlit, hung at heights that need a ladder you do not own.

Whole-house design

Not sure what it should look like? We will walk it, sketch a plan and quote two versions — a clean one and a bigger one.

How the season runs

1

August – October · quote and book

We look at the house, agree the plan and lock a date. The calendar fills from the north beaches down, and most installs are booked by mid-October.

2

November · install

Lights are measured and cut for your house, then installed. You do not need to be home, and you do not need to own anything.

3

Through the season · it stays lit

A run goes dark, a squirrel gets ambitious, a nor'easter arrives — we come back and fix it. Included.

4

January · down and stored

We remove everything, label it and store it. Next year is a phone call, and it goes up faster.

Why not just do it yourself

Plenty of people should. If it is a single-storey ranch and you like the job, have at it. Call us when one of these is true:

  • The roofline is two or three storeys, steep, or over a deck you would not want to fall onto.
  • You are only here some weekends and the lights need to work when you are not.
  • It is a rental and the turnover schedule does not care about your weekend plans.
  • You have done it for years and have decided this is the year you stop.
