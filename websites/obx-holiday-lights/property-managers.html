Why it is worth doing

Lit homes photograph better

A decorated exterior gives you fresh listing photography for the one season your inventory competes hardest, and it is the cheapest exterior upgrade on the property.

Families booking a beach house at Christmas are buying a Christmas, not a bed count. Lights are the visible part of that.
