The drivers

What the price is built from

  • Linear feet of roofline. The main driver. We measure it rather than guess.
  • Height and pitch. A single-storey gutter run and a third-storey dormer are not the same job, and the second one is why you are calling.
  • Trees and landscape. Priced per tree by size, because a crepe myrtle and a live oak are different afternoons.
  • Columns, railings and docks. Priced by run.
  • Access. Tight lots, soft sand, and anything that means carrying gear a long way.
No extras

What is always included

  • The lights themselves — commercial-grade LED, cut to your house. You are not buying them, and you are not storing them.
  • Installation and timers.
  • Maintenance all season. If it goes dark, we come back. Not a service call, not an extra.
  • Takedown in January and storage until next year.
In writing, then fixed

How the quote works

1

We look at the house

In person where we can, from your photos and the parcel where we cannot. It takes about fifteen minutes.

2

You get it in writing

A fixed price for a described scope. Usually two versions — a clean one and a bigger one — so you can pick.

3

The price does not move

If we misjudged the roof, that is our problem. The only thing that changes a written price is you asking for more lights.

Year two and on

Repeat years cost less

The first season carries the measuring, the cutting and the design. After that the lights already exist and already fit, so returning customers pay less for the same house. It is also why we store them rather than handing them back.
