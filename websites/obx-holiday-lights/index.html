The deal

What you actually get

We supply the lights

Commercial-grade LED strands cut to your house, not big-box strings stapled end to end. They stay with us between seasons, so you are not storing eleven tangled boxes in a garage.

We do the roof

Steep coastal rooflines, third-storey dormers and dock railings are the whole reason people call. We are insured, and nobody in your family goes up a ladder in November wind.

It stays lit

If a run goes dark during the season we come back and fix it. That is included, not a service call. Salt air kills cheap lights and connections — that is our problem, not yours.

It comes down

Takedown in January is part of the price, not an upsell you forget to book. We pull it, label it, and store it for next year.
