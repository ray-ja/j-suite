Straight answers

What people ask before they book

Do you haul the debris away?

Yes — always, and it's in the price unless we tell you otherwise. We run our own dump trailer, so the brush, limbs and wood leave with us the same day. You should never be left with a pile to deal with.

Can you quote from a photo?

For most trimming, brush and small-tree work, yes — text a picture with a rough idea of the area and we'll give you a number the same day. For full lot clearing or a big removal near a house we'll come walk it, free.

Are you insured?

Yes, general liability, and we'll send you the certificate on request. Tree work near a house is exactly the wrong place to hire someone who isn't.

How big a tree will you take?

We take trees we can drop and rig safely — which covers most of what's on the Banks. If a tree needs a crane or a climb we're not equipped for, we'll tell you straight and point you to someone who is, rather than take a risk with your house.

Do you grind the stump too?

We can. It's priced separately because plenty of people only want the tree down, so you can take just the removal or have us grind it below grade and leave it ready to plant or mow.

How soon can you get here?

Usually the same week, and often within a day or two for small jobs. After a storm we run a priority list — call early.
