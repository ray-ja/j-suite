What we can't take

We'd rather tell you up front than surprise you on the day.

  • Hazardous waste — paint, solvents, chemicals, asbestos
  • Propane tanks, fuel & oil drums
  • Dirt, sand, rock & concrete (too heavy for our trailer)
  • Titled vehicles, boats & trailers
  • Medical or biohazard waste
  • Anything actively leaking or smouldering
