The short list we can't take — and where it can go

Rules keep a few things off our trailer, but none of them are dead ends. Here's the route for each one.

Oil-based paint, solvents & chemicals WE ROUTE IT

Hazardous-waste rules keep these off our trailer. Both counties run a free residents' collection day each spring — Currituck around April, Dare each May — that takes oil paints, thinners, pool chemicals and pesticides. Your county's Public Works department has the dates.

Propane tanks, fuel & oil drums WE ROUTE IT

Grill tanks: swap at any hardware-store tank exchange, or the county collection days take them. Used motor oil & antifreeze: Currituck convenience centers and most auto-parts stores take them free, year-round.

Loose dirt, sand & rock WE ROUTE IT

Concrete slabs & walls we take — broken up and weighed at the scale (demolition). Clean fill dirt: local grading and landscaping outfits often want it — worth a post before you pay anyone.

Titled vehicles & vehicle trailers WE ROUTE IT

A licensed junk-car buyer or salvage yard will usually tow it free and sort the title paperwork with you; many pay cash for it.

Medical or biohazard waste WE ROUTE IT

Old medications: pharmacy take-back kiosks. Sharps: sealed in a rigid container per county guidance — the health department can point you to a drop-off.
