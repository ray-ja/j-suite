How it works

One clean install, then it's just an app

Individually addressable low-voltage LEDs sit in a slim track color-matched to your trim, so in daylight the system all but disappears. At night you pick the look: classic warm white, full holiday color, or an accent scene you build yourself.

Every run is measured to your roofline, wired to a controller inside, and sealed for salt air. Because it is permanent, there is no putting lights up in a 25-mph November wind and no pulling staples out of your fascia every January.

Schedules handle it after that — on at dusk, off at midnight, holiday palettes ready when the season comes around.
