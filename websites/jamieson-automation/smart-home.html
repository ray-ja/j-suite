FAQ

Common questions

Which smart lock is best for a vacation rental?

For most OBX rentals a Wi-Fi keypad deadbolt with automatic guest codes is ideal. If wifi is weak at the door we add coverage or use a hub-based lock.

Why does my smart lock keep going offline?

Almost always it is weak wifi at the door, not the lock. The fix is proper coverage with a mesh node or access point near the entry.

Should I choose PoE or WiFi cameras?

For four or more cameras we recommend PoE (wired) systems that record to a local NVR — no monthly fee, higher resolution, and far more reliable than wifi cameras.

Do your camera systems have monthly fees?

Our recommended PoE systems record locally to an NVR with no required subscription. You own your footage and avoid recurring cloud fees.

Do you do electrical work?

We handle low-voltage work: locks, cameras, networking, Starlink, thermostats, and lighting. For line-voltage electrical we bring in a licensed electrician partner.
