Our story

A technician's obsession with things that just work.

Jamieson Networks is Ray Jamieson — a working technician, not a franchise. Ray's background is commercial AV and control systems: Q-SYS audio systems, PLC control work, and the networks underneath them — installations where a dropped connection stops a business, not just a movie. He started the company to bring that standard to Outer Banks homes and rentals.

Being a one-person company is the point, not a limitation. The person who quotes your job is the person on the ladder, and the person who answers the phone afterwards. Nothing gets lost between a salesman, a scheduler, and a subcontractor — because there aren't any.

Here's the thing most installers miss: everything in the house is only as good as the network behind it. That's why we lead with networking. We diagnose the whole system, fix the coverage, and only then install anything on top of it — so it doesn't become a callback three weeks later.

We're local, we're independent, and we're not here to push the cheapest box. We're here to design the right system, explain it in plain English, and be the person you call when you want it done properly.
