Our story

A technician's obsession with things that just work.

Our founder is a hands-on technician with deep roots in automation, audio-video, and networking — the kind of person who takes things apart to understand them from first principles. After years working on complex systems where failure wasn't an option, he started Jamieson Automation to bring that same standard to Outer Banks homes and rentals.

Here's the thing most installers miss: a smart lock or camera is only as good as the network behind it. That's why we lead with networking. We diagnose the whole system, fix the wifi at the door, and only then install the device — so it doesn't become a callback three weeks later.

We're local, we're independent, and we're not here to push the cheapest box. We're here to design the right system, explain it in plain English, and be the person you call when you want it done properly.
