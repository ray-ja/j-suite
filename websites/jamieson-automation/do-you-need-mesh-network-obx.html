Do You Need a Mesh Network on the Outer Banks?

Weak wifi breaks everything that depends on it: locks, cameras, streaming, and Starlink. A single router struggles in larger or multi-floor coastal homes, and dead zones appear right where you need coverage.

A mesh network blankets the whole place in signal; for bigger properties or commercial, UniFi access points are the rock-solid choice. It is the foundation we build every smart-home on.

  • Over ~1,500 sq ft → mesh
  • Big / multi-unit / commercial → UniFi
  • Coverage first, devices second
