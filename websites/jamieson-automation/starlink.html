The mount Starlink doesn't sell

Hurricane-rated pole mounts, built for Dare County wind

Here's what nobody tells you at checkout: Starlink publishes no wind rating for the Standard dish, and Starlink's own Ridgeline roof mount is rated to just 50 mph. The Outer Banks sees named storms most years — 50 mph is a breezy Tuesday in November, not a hurricane.

Our premium option is a mount we engineer for the site: a rigid steel pole, through-bolted to the structure or set in its own concrete footing, sized and placed for the wind exposure of your property. The dish stays put, stays aligned, and doesn't become a projectile in the first real blow.

Nobody else on the beach is offering this. If your Starlink is going on an exposed oceanfront home in Rodanthe or a tall pole in Corolla, this is the difference between re-buying a dish every storm season and forgetting it's up there.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to buy the Starlink kit myself?

Yes — you buy the kit (about $349) directly from Starlink. We handle professional installation, mounting, weather sealing, and whole-home wifi.

How much does Starlink installation cost?

A standard install starts around $299. Roof pivot mounts run about $449 and pole mounts about $499. Mesh wifi add-ons are quoted per property.

What mount types do you offer?

Standard (kickstand/eave/ground), roof pivot mount for slanted shingle roofs, pole or mast mounts for tricky sky views — and our premium engineered hurricane-rated pole mount, through-bolted or concrete-footed for storm wind.

Will a Starlink mount survive a hurricane here?

Not the stock options: Starlink publishes no wind rating for the Standard dish, and its Ridgeline mount is rated to only 50 mph. Our engineered pole mount — through-bolted or set in a concrete footing — is built for the wind Dare County actually gets.

Will Starlink reach the whole house?

We add mesh wifi nodes where needed so the signal covers every room — the kit's router alone often will not cover a larger home or rental.

Is Starlink good for areas with poor cable internet?

Yes. Starlink is ideal for OBX rentals and homes where cable internet is slow or unavailable, as long as there is a clear view of the sky.
