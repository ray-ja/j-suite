FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to buy the Starlink kit myself?

Yes — you buy the kit (about $349) directly from Starlink. We handle professional installation, mounting, weather sealing, and whole-home wifi.

How much does Starlink installation cost?

A standard install starts around $299. Roof pivot mounts run about $449 and pole mounts about $499. Mesh wifi add-ons are quoted per property.

What mount types do you offer?

Standard (kickstand/eave/ground), roof pivot mount for slanted shingle roofs, and pole or mast mounts for tricky sky views.

Will Starlink reach the whole house?

We add mesh wifi nodes where needed so the signal covers every room — the kit's router alone often will not cover a larger home or rental.

Is Starlink good for areas with poor cable internet?

Yes. Starlink is ideal for OBX rentals and homes where cable internet is slow or unavailable, as long as there is a clear view of the sky.
