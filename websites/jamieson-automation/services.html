How our pricing works

You pay a flat, published rate for the install — our skilled labor is the real product. Equipment is billed at our cost, with no markup; you’re welcome to buy it yourself and we’ll install it. You’re paying for the expertise that makes it work right the first time, not for marked-up gear.

Starlink installation

The Starlink kit is easy to buy and hard to place well. We find the clear sky view, run a clean weather-sealed cable path, and extend the wifi so it covers the whole property — not just the room with the router.

The mount matters here. Starlink publishes no wind rating for the Standard dish, and their own Ridgeline mount is rated to only 50 mph — not enough for Dare County. Our premium option is an engineered pole mount, through-bolted to structure or set in its own concrete footing, built for hurricane-zone wind.

Install from $299 · roof/pole mounts $449–$499 · hurricane-rated engineered pole mount quoted per site · cable runs from $59 · Starlink kit bought direct (~$349), never marked up
