# Marketing Channel Plan — every avenue, ranked by cost & payback

*You're low on funds, so this is organized by what it costs, not by channel type. Tier 0 is free/sweat-equity — do all of it now. Each tier up costs more; the table tells you the cost, the eyes, and the realistic leads so you know what to turn on as cash comes in — and what to prepare for. Numbers are 2025–26 industry benchmarks; the OBX is rural/low-competition, so your costs trend to the low end.*

**The one rule when broke:** spend sweat before dollars, and spend your first dollars where you only pay for a *result* (a lead), not for *impressions*. That ordering is baked in below.

## TIER 0 — FREE / sweat equity (do ALL of this now, both businesses)

| Channel | What it is | Cost | Eyes / reach | Realistic result | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile (GBP)** | Your free listing in Google Maps + the "local pack" | **$0** | Most local-service customers start here; ~46% of Google searches are local | The single highest-ROI free asset. Ranks you on "pressure washing near me." **Get reviews** — they drive the ranking | 1–2 hrs setup, then ask every customer |
| **Direct B2B outreach (OBX)** | Cold call + in-person drop-bys to property managers/HOAs (your call list) | **$0** | 20+ PMs, each = dozens of doors | **Your #1 channel.** One PM yes = recurring revenue across many properties | Your time — highest payoff per hour |
| **Referrals + review asks** | Ask every happy customer for a referral + a Google review | **$0** | Warm, pre-trusted | Highest *close rate* of any channel | 30 seconds per job |
| **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups** | Free posts with before/after photos in OBX community groups | **$0** | Thousands of locals/owners | Trusted neighbor recommendations; great for washing/house-watch | A few posts/week |
| **Door hangers (OBX)** | Flyers on doors in target neighborhoods | **~$0.12/each print** (your brothers distribute = free labor) | 500 hangers → ~500 homes | 1–3% response = **5–15 calls per 500**; CPA $60–120 if you paid for delivery — near-zero for you | Drop while on-site/between jobs |
| **Branded shirts + truck magnets** | Uniform + magnetic door signs | **~$100–200 one-time** | Every job site + every drive | Cheap rolling visibility now, before a full wrap | One-time |
| **Yard signs** | Sign at every job you do | **~$5–10/sign** | Neighbors of every customer | "Who did your driveway?" — neighborhood clustering | Plant on site |

**Jamieson note:** same list, but your B2B outreach targets **vacation-rental managers** (Starlink/wifi/locks per door) and your GBP/website ranks you for "OBX Starlink installer," "home automation OBX." Your website (already built) is your SEO foundation — publish it.

## TIER 1 — FIRST DOLLARS (under ~$300/mo) — turn on as soon as any cash flows

| Channel | What it is | Cost | Eyes / reach | Realistic result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Local Services Ads (LSA)** ⭐ | "Google Guaranteed" badge at the very top; **you pay per lead, not per click** | **$12–30 per lead** (cleaning/washing is among the cheapest); set a weekly budget, e.g. **$200–300/mo** | Top-of-page on local searches | At $20/lead, **$250/mo ≈ 12 leads**; cleaning converts **30–40%** → **4–5 jobs/mo**, and a recurring client at $250/mo = $3,000/yr. **This is your best first paid dollar.** |
| **Keep the $10/day Google Search** (you already run it) | Standard search ads | **~$300/mo at $10/day**; CPC ~$1–2 | Search-intent traffic | Decent, but **shift budget to LSA first** — LSA pays per lead, not per click |

**Why LSA first:** you only pay when a real customer contacts you, the badge builds instant trust (huge when "reliable" is your pitch), and cleaning has some of the best LSA economics of any trade. ([LSA cost-per-lead data](https://www.themediacaptain.com/google-local-service-ad-statistics/))

**Jamieson:** LSA has categories for some home-tech/security work; otherwise your Tier-1 dollar is a **small Google Search budget** on high-intent terms ("OBX Starlink install," "smart lock installer OBX") — buyers there are ready to hire.

## TIER 2 — SCALE (~$300–1,500/mo) — when recurring revenue is covering the bills

| Channel | What it is | Cost | Eyes / reach | Realistic result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta ads (Facebook/Instagram)** | Targeted local ads + lead forms; before/after creative | **$5–10/day min; $300–800/mo** to start; CPC $0.92–1.92, **CPL ~$18–22**, CPM ~$16 (small buys $4–7) | Highly targetable by zip/interest | $400/mo ≈ **18–22 leads**; best for residential washing, holiday lighting, and high-ticket Jamieson installs |
| **Google Search PPC (scaled)** | Bid on more service terms | **$500–1,000/mo** | Ready-to-buy searchers | Predictable lead flow once LSA is maxed |
| **Vehicle wrap** ⭐ | Full truck wrap | **$2,500–5,000 one-time, lasts 5–7 yrs** | **30,000–70,000 impressions/day**, **$0.36 CPM — the cheapest advertising that exists** | Most businesses recoup in 3–6 months; in a town you drive constantly, this is a no-brainer once you have the cash |
| **EDDM (direct mail)** | USPS "every door" to chosen carrier routes | **$0.30–0.60/piece; 5,000 = $1,500–2,600** | 5,000 targeted homes | ~1% response = **~50 responses**; CPA $80–150. Target **second-home/rental zips** for house-watch + washing |

## TIER 3 — BIGGER / LATER (prepare for, don't fund yet)

- **Full local SEO build-out** (content, service-area pages, backlinks) — ~$500–1,500/mo or DIY; comparable booked job runs ~$290–310 (far cheaper than lead platforms long-term). The compounding, durable channel.
- **Retargeting funnels** (re-show ads to site visitors) — cheap add-on once you have ad traffic.
- **Local sponsorships / chamber / events** — brand trust in a small community.
- **Fleet wraps** (multiple trucks) and **billboard** (only if a key route justifies it).
- **A part-time marketer / agency** — only once revenue clearly funds it.

---

## ❌ AVOID (especially while broke): Angi & Thumbtack

The data is damning for a tight budget: **the same lead is sold to 3–5 contractors**, ~**70% of Angi leads don't answer or aren't qualified**, real customer-acquisition cost runs **$100–250+ (Thumbtack)** up to **~$2,500 (Angi)**, and contractor reviews are brutal ("I made about 15% of what I spent"). Your free direct outreach and LSA beat these on every metric. Skip them. ([lead-platform cost analysis](https://talk24.ai/blog/home-services-platform-lead-costs))

## THE PLAN, in plain order

**This week ($0):** Set up **Google Business Profile** for both businesses and start collecting reviews. Work the **PM call list** (your #1 channel). Post **before/after photos** to Nextdoor + local FB groups. Print cheap **door hangers** and drop them between jobs. Get **truck magnets + shirts**.

**First spare ~$250/mo:** Turn on **Google Local Services Ads** for OBX washing/cleanup (pay-per-lead, Google Guaranteed). For Jamieson, a small **Search budget** on "OBX Starlink/smart-lock install."

**When recurring revenue covers the bills (~$500–1,500/mo):** Add **Meta ads** (before/after creative), scale **Search/LSA**, and get the **vehicle wrap** (cheapest impressions on earth). Test **EDDM** into second-home/rental zips for house-watch.

**Prepare for (Tier 3):** a real **local-SEO** build-out — it's the durable, compounding channel that eventually makes paid ads optional.

**Per-business emphasis:**
- **OBX Lot Solutions** → win on **free B2B outreach + GBP + LSA + door hangers**, because property managers and recurring residential washing are local and trust-driven.
- **Jamieson Automation** → win on **GBP + website SEO + referrals + targeted search**, plus **cross-sell through the same property managers** OBX Lot Solutions is landing. Premium tech is relationship- and search-intent-driven, not impression-driven.
