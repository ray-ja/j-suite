## SMART LOCKS — the one you asked about

**First question on every lock job: how does it talk, and how does it mount?** Those two answers drive everything.

### Connectivity (how it talks) — this is the big one

| Type | How it works | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wi-Fi (built-in)** | Lock joins the home wifi directly | Remote control + auto guest codes with **no hub**; simplest | Heavier battery drain (6–12 mo); needs **good wifi at the door** | Most vacation rentals — the default |
| **Z-Wave / Zigbee** | Talks to a **hub/bridge**, not wifi | Best **battery life** and **scales** to many doors on one dashboard | Requires a hub ($50–150); more setup | Multi-unit / many-door PMs |
| **Bluetooth only** | Phone-to-lock, short range | Cheap | **No remote access** | Owner-occupied, not rentals |
| **Matter/Thread** | Newer universal standard | Future-proof, cross-brand | Still maturing | Forward-looking installs |
| **Offline PIN (igloohome)** | Codes generated by algorithm, **no internet** | Works where wifi is bad | No live remote monitoring | Rentals with poor connectivity |

**Rule of thumb for OBX rentals:** built-in **Wi-Fi** lock unless (a) wifi is weak at the door → add a mesh node or use Z-Wave+hub, or (b) the PM runs many doors → Z-Wave/Zigbee on a hub scales better.

### How it mounts (physical type)

- **Full smart deadbolt** (Yale, Schlage) — replaces the whole deadbolt; keypad + motor. Cleanest look, most features.
- **Retrofit / interior-only** (August) — keeps the **existing deadbolt and keys**; you only swap the inside thumb-turn. **10-minute install**, landlord/HOA-friendly, great when you can't change the exterior. Pair with a $50 keypad for codes.
- **Lever vs deadbolt** — most rentals want a deadbolt; levers for interior/secondary doors.

### Brands & where they fit (2025–26)

| Lock | Connectivity | ~Hardware | Why pick it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yale Assure Lock 2** | swappable module: Wi-Fi / Z-Wave / Zigbee / Matter | $250–300 | **Best overall for rentals** — native Airbnb integration, auto codes, reliable |
| **Schlage Encode / Encode Plus** | built-in Wi-Fi (Plus adds Apple Home Key) | $250–300 | **Most secure** (Grade 1), 3-yr warranty |
| **August Wi-Fi** | Wi-Fi (retrofit) | $230 + $50 keypad | **Keeps existing key**, condo/HOA-friendly, fastest install |
| **Ultraloq U-Bolt Pro Wi-Fi** | Wi-Fi (+hub option) | ~$189 | **Budget** with fingerprint; 6 unlock methods |
| **igloohome** | offline PIN | ~$200 | **No wifi needed** — bad-connectivity rentals |

Battery: most last 6–12 months (August 3–6). Always set the PM's expectation on battery swaps — or sell a check on it.

### What an install actually includes (don't just sell the lock)

Quote these as the job, not just the hardware:
1. **The lock** (supply, or customer-supplied).
2. **Mounting/swap** + door fit check (older OBX doors are often out of square).
3. **Wi-Fi at the door** — verify signal; **add a mesh node** if weak (this is the #1 callback cause).
4. **Hub/bridge** if Z-Wave/Zigbee.
5. **App + account setup**, firmware update.
6. **PIN/code scheme + PMS integration** (Airbnb/Vrbo, or RemoteLock/Seam for auto codes) — **this is billable and where you add value.**
7. **Handoff**: how the PM/owner manages codes, battery, lockouts.

**Quote lines to use:** `Smart lock — WiFi deadbolt, supply + install` ($379) or `retrofit` ($279) or `install only` ($129); add `Z-Wave/Zigbee hub` ($149) and `Lock PIN/PMS setup per lock` ($39). For weak wifi, add a `WiFi access point` or `Mesh WiFi` line. ([Smart lock comparison source](https://smartlockmfg.com/best-smart-locks-of-2026-expert-testing-and-reviews/))

## STARLINK

**Kit ($349, customer buys direct from Starlink):** Gen 3 dish, Wi-Fi 6 router, kickstand, ~49 ft cable, power supply. **The kit does NOT include a permanent mount.** ([Starlink](https://www.satelliteinternet.com/providers/starlink/))

What the install is really about: **a clear view of the sky + a clean cable path inside.**
- **Standard** ($299): kickstand/eave/ground placement, simple cable pass-through.
- **Roof pivot mount** ($449): the [pivot mount](https://www.homedepot.com/p/STARLINK-Pivot-Mount-Standard-Kit-V4-04759105/330646164) for slanted/shingle roofs (~$50 part), clean cable run + sealed entry.
- **Pole/mast** ($499): pipe adapter (fits 1.25–2.5" pole).
- Add **cable extension** ($59/50 ft) for long runs (Starlink sells a 150-ft cable).
- Coverage: if the router can't cover the whole rental, **add a mesh node** — common upsell.

**Sell it as a package:** Starlink install + mesh wifi + a smart lock = the "rental-ready" bundle.

## CAMERAS & SECURITY

**The fork: PoE (wired) vs Wi-Fi.**

- **PoE (wired, NVR):** one Cat5e/Cat6 cable carries power + data; records to a local **NVR** with **no monthly fee**; higher res (up to 12 MP); far more reliable. Costs more upfront/labor. Reolink 4K 4-cam kits ~$300–600; Lorex NVR ~$430 + cameras. ([Reolink guide](https://reolink.com/blog/nvr-security-camera-system-buying-guide/))
- **Wi-Fi (Ring/Arlo/Reolink wifi):** cheaper hardware, fast install, but usually **monthly fees**, lower res, drops under load.

**Recommend PoE for anyone with 4+ cameras** — it breaks even in year 1–2 vs subscription cameras and is far more reliable. **UniFi Protect** for the IT-savvy / commercial (your networking strength).

What the install includes: cameras, NVR, **Cat6 runs** (price per run), PoE switch or NVR-with-PoE, mounting, configuration, app + remote viewing, motion zones.
**Quote lines:** `PoE 4-cam 4K NVR kit, supply+install` ($1,299) + `additional PoE camera` ($249) + `Cat6 camera run` ($89/run) + `NVR setup` ($149).

## NETWORKING & WI-FI (your home turf)

Most "my smart lock/camera dropped" calls are really **wifi coverage** problems — so networking underpins everything.

- **Mesh wifi** (consumer, up to 3 nodes) — $499 supply+install; good for most homes/rentals.
- **UniFi** (your specialty) — access points ($99–199 each), a gateway/router, PoE switch, Cat6 drops. Premium, rock-solid, the right call for big homes, multi-unit, and commercial.
- **Cat6 drops** — price per run; pre-terminated 25/50/100 ft saves time.
- Always sell **network design + config** ($149) — it's the difference between "works" and "works reliably."

## SMART HOME & CLIMATE

- **Smart thermostat** (low-voltage, no electrician needed) — supply+install $279, or install-only $149. Big for rentals (remote temp control between guests).
- **Smart switches/dimmers** — ⚠️ **line-voltage = licensed electrician.** Stick to low-voltage/plug-in, or partner out. (Battery devices, thermostats, locks, cameras, networking are all fine for you.)
- **Hub** (Home Assistant — your first-principles wheelhouse — or SmartThings) to tie it together: $199 setup.

## OUTDOOR LIGHTING (the high-ticket bet)

Permanent LED systems (Trimlight/Jellyfish/Gemstone style): app-controlled, **year-round** (holiday + accent + security).
- **~$25/linear ft installed**, **controller + setup ~$650**, **starter (up to 100 ft) ~$2,200**. ([Jellyfish pricing](https://www.jellyfishlighting.com/pricing))
- It's low-voltage + network/app config = your wheelhouse. Become a **dealer for one brand**, build a demo, sell into the fall.

## THE GOLDEN RULES FOR QUOTING JAMIESON WORK

1. **Never sell just the device — sell the working outcome.** Lock + wifi-at-the-door + code setup. Camera + cable + NVR + app. That's why your quotes are higher *and* fairer than a box-store DIY.
2. **Wi-Fi is the foundation.** Check signal first; add coverage when needed. It prevents 80% of callbacks.
3. **Stay on the right side of the license line.** Low-voltage, network, battery, plug-in, mounting = you. Line-voltage panel/hardwire = licensed electrician (partner).
4. **Bundle for rentals:** Starlink/wifi + smart lock + thermostat + cameras = one "rental-ready" package sold per door to property managers.
5. **Your labor is the product.** Hardware is near pass-through; price your **time and expertise** (tech labor $125/hr baseline) — it's worth 2.4–3.7× your old $55/hr.
