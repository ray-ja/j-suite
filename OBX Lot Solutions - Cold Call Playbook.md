# OBX Lot Solutions — Cold Call Playbook

**Goal of every call:** not to sell — to book a free 5-minute walkthrough so you can give a quote. Easy yes.

**Your edge, say it every time:** *reliable, professional, shows up.* In OBX that's rare and everyone knows it. That's why people will switch to you.

**Best time to call:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am or 1:30–4pm. Avoid Monday morning and Friday afternoon.

---

## The opener (Tier 1 & 2 — property managers, HOAs, shopping centers)

> "Hi, this is **Ray with OBX Lot Solutions** — we handle parking-lot, walkway, and dumpster-area cleanup for commercial and HOA properties here on the Outer Banks. Are you the right person to talk to about keeping your properties' common areas clean?"

If yes → go to **the pitch**. If no → *"No problem — who would I talk to about that?"* (get name + extension, log it).

## The pitch (15 seconds, then stop talking)

> "We keep lots, sidewalks, entries, and dumpster areas free of trash and debris on a recurring schedule — so your properties always look cared-for and you never get a complaint about it. We're local, uniformed, and reliable, and recurring accounts get **20% off**. Could I swing by one of your properties this week, take five minutes to look, and put together a no-pressure quote?"

Then **be quiet** and let them answer.

## The ask

- "What day works — would Wednesday or Thursday be better?"
- Lock a time. Get the property address and their cell/email for the quote.
- "Great — I'll be there [day/time] in uniform. You'll have a written quote same day."

---

## Tier 3 — Landscapers (PARTNER call, different angle)

> "Hi, this is Ray with OBX Lot Solutions. I'm not trying to sell you anything — I actually want to make your commercial accounts easier. You handle the grounds; we handle the stuff you probably *don't* want to mess with — litter pickup, dumpster-area cleanup, lot debris, post-storm trash. If a client ever needs that, we can be your reliable sub or you refer us and we take care of your people. Worth grabbing coffee for ten minutes?"

This turns a competitor into a feeder. One yes can hand you several properties.

---

## Objection handling

**"We already have someone."**
> "Totally fair. Most people I talk to do — until that someone no-shows. Can I leave you a quote anyway so you've got a reliable backup the next time they don't show? Costs you nothing to have it on file."

**"How much?"**
> "Depends on the size and how often — that's why I'd rather look first so I'm not guessing. Five minutes on-site and you'll have an exact number, no obligation. Recurring gets 20% off."

**"We don't have budget."**
> "Understood. The recurring discount is built for exactly that — and a clean property prevents the complaints and code issues that cost a lot more. Let me quote it so you can decide with a real number."

**"Send me something / email me."**
> "Happy to drop off a one-pager in person so you can put a face to the name — I'm in [their town] this week anyway. What's the best time?" *(In-person beats email here — it shows reliability and lets you eyeball the property.)*

**"Call me back later / not now."**
> "No problem — when's a better time, later this week or early next?" *(Get a specific day. Log it. Actually call back.)*

---

## After every call — log it in the Status / Notes column

Use a quick code:
- **W** = walkthrough booked (+ day/time)
- **CB** = call back (+ date)
- **Q** = quote sent
- **NO** = not interested (+ why)
- **GK** = gatekeeper, need decision-maker (+ name)
