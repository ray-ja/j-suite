## The close sequence

**1 · One-pager — open the conversation**
`proposals/Rental-Ready Tech - Package One-Pager.pdf`
The leave-behind / send-ahead. Gets the prospect to see the three tiers and the value before you quote anything. *Customer-facing.*

**2 · Proposal — put it in front of them**
`proposals/Jamieson - PM Rental-Ready Proposal (TEMPLATE).docx`
Fill in the tier, quantities, and price; this is the signable document with the Investment Summary and DocuSign anchors. *Customer-facing.*

**3 · BOM — scope it and protect margin**
`proposals/Rental-Ready Tech - Hardware BOM & Margin Model.xlsx`
Your internal quoting tool — confirm hardware cost + markup and see the margin before you commit a number to the proposal. *Internal.*

**4 · Portfolio playbook — when it's a multi-door PM deal**
`sales/PM Portfolio & Volume-Deal Playbook.md` + `sales/PM Volume-Deal Calculator.xlsx`
For property managers with multiple doors: the volume-break structure, the consolidated-vendor pitch, and the calculator to model the whole portfolio (deal total, savings, recurring, your time saved). *Internal playbook; calculator output is shareable.*

**5 · Commitment — lock the volume deal**
`sales/Jamieson - PM Portfolio Agreement.docx`  ✅ *attorney-approved, client-ready*
The signable contract that locks the committed door count to unlock volume pricing. Use this when a PM commits the book. *Customer-facing.*

**6 · Comms — move it forward at every step**
`client files/Jamieson - Client Communication Templates.docx`
Nine email/SMS drafts spanning the whole flow — inquiry reply, proposal send + nudge, scheduling, handoff, review request, support, renewal. Pull the right one at each stage. *Customer-facing (after you personalize).*

## After the close (hand off to delivery)

The sale is done — these run the job and the relationship (see `README.md` for detail):

- `install docs/Jamieson - Install & Support Checklist.docx` — run the install + handoff.
- `install docs/Jamieson - Warranty Tracker.xlsx` — log every device; its expiry flags trigger renewal outreach (comms template 5b).

---

## Quick reference — what's customer-facing vs internal

**Hand to the client:** one-pager, proposal, portfolio agreement, (personalized) comms, calculator output.
**Keep internal:** pitch & objection guide, BOM/margin model, the playbook itself.
