# OBX competitive market — what the local field actually looks like
_Research run 2026-08-08. Every claim below carries a source URL. Where a price could not be found, that is stated rather than estimated._

## The three findings that change what we do first

**1. There is no Outer Banks-based holiday lighting installer.** The only companies serving the OBX drive in:
- **Stateline Christmas Lights** (Moyock) — packages **start at $800**, tiers $800 / $800–2,300 / $2,300+, maintenance through Dec 23, January takedown and storage, **10% off October installs**. https://statelinechristmaslights.com/f/christmas-light-installations
- **Coastal Christmas Decor** (Virginia Beach) — residential **from $1,500**, colour-changing from $1,800, permanent roof from $1,800. Their own site lists only Hampton Roads cities; OBX coverage is **unconfirmed**. https://coastalchristmasdecor.com/starting-prices/

Nobody owns this market locally. "The local guy who is actually here in December when a strand dies" beats a crew ninety minutes away, and that is a positioning line we can use verbatim.

**2. The booking window closes sooner than the season suggests.** Industry norm: installers fill **8–12 weeks before** the season; by October most quality installers are booked; November booking carries 20–40% premiums. Stateline's October discount confirms the local pattern.
→ **Marketing must be live by early September, quoting through October.** Sources: https://curbstead.com/guides/when-to-book · https://www.christmaslightsexpertsdfw.com/blog-when-to-book

**3. Q-SYS / commercial AV has zero local competition.** No Q-SYS presence found on the OBX or in Elizabeth City. The nearest real commercial-AV shops are Norfolk / Virginia Beach (AVI-SPL, EHI, Loyola, VIcom) — enterprise firms that will not chase a small OBX conference room two hours away. This is the one line where the undercut framing does not even apply.

## The one thing this research argues on its own
**Almost nobody in the local tech-services field publishes a price.** Publishing transparent pricing is therefore itself a differentiator here, and it happens to be exactly the undercut/value brand we already decided on.
