## The visit
**20-30 minutes on site** (published repeatedly) — expect the long end for 6-8BR cottages — **plus ~15 min drive** on a zone-clustered route → **8-10 visits per field day**, ~12 if tightly clustered.

Checklist: exterior walk-around · every room · run faucets, flush all toilets · under sinks · water heater · HVAC + thermostat · fridge/freezer · washing-machine hoses · water intrusion, mould, pests · breaker panel · humidity · relock · photo documentation · emailed report. Coastal extras: humidity/mildew, storm-surge level, hot tub and pool equipment.

⚠️ **Competitors freely say "inspection," "security," "patrol."** One literally brands "Premier Secure House Checking." **We do not** — same content, rendered as observations. "Home Watch Visit" and "Visit Report," never inspection/patrol/security check. They are carrying licensing risk we have chosen not to.

## 💰 THE UNIT ECONOMICS
Modelled mix (40% monthly, 45% bi-weekly, 15% weekly) → **~$111 per home per month**, ~1.9 visits/home/month.

| MRR target | Homes needed | Field days/month |
|---|---|---|
| **$3,000** | **~27 homes** | ~6 |
| **$5,000** | **~45 homes** | ~10 |
| **$8,000** | **~72 homes** | ~15 |

**Effective rate: ~$93/hr gross, ~$86/hr after mileage — versus the ~$18/hr the field work has actually been netting.**

**Even $8k MRR is only ~15 field-days a month for ONE person.** Home watch never saturates a 2-man crew. It is a route business that coexists with lights, junk and Starlink — and feeds them leads through the vendor-meet add-on.

**Storm events are additive and large:** one hurricane touching 45 client homes at $95 prep + $85 post-storm ≈ **$8,100 in a week.** ⚠️ Modelled; per-event pricing evidence is thin.

## 🔴 THE REAL RISK IS CLIENT ACQUISITION
> *"Client acquisition, not capacity or pricing, is the risk."*

No operator publishes client counts. **27/45/72 homes is arithmetic, not evidence.** Ray has had **4 customers ever.** Going from 4 to 45 is the entire game — the pricing and the capacity are the easy parts.

**Channel:** the property-manager channel is **selling to a competitor** here — Shoreline already runs its own owner programme. The defensible route is **direct-to-owner** (true second homes, plus rental owners unhappy with off-season coverage) with **realtors and closing attorneys as referrers.** Off-season PM service quality is a live homeowner complaint locally.

## Insurance — one detail that matters more than the rest
**Standard General Liability EXCLUDES damage to property in your care.** That is precisely an empty house you hold the keys to. Required: **GL + Professional Liability (E&O) + a CARE, CUSTODY & CONTROL endorsement**, plus a **lost-key** endorsement and a bond for theft claims.
⚠️ Home-watch premiums are quote-only. Small-business GL averages $45/mo ($265-3,030/yr). Budget $1,000-2,500/yr **as a placeholder pending real quotes.** Get CCC + lost-key confirmed **in writing before taking the first key.**

## NHWA
**First year $595** (a $185 guide credits toward it). Requires background checks, business records, evidence of insurance and bond, annual CE. Renewal dues and exact insurance minimums **not published — flagged.** No evidence accredited operators charge more — but **Cottage Comforts, a direct competitor, advertises it**, so it is a live trust signal in this exact market. "Insured and bonded" matters more than the logo.

## Software — what the app must do
Benchmark **Home Watch IT $59-109/mo**, so owning j-Suite saves ~$700-1,400/yr. Table stakes, and the third one IS the product:
1. Per-property checklist templates, per-item OK / photo / note
2. **GPS + timestamp stamped on every photo and visit record** — this is the proof-of-visit and the insurance-grade documentation
3. Auto-generated **Visit Report** emailed on completion (observations only)
4. Issue flagging with follow-up · visit history per property · **offline-first capture** (cell gaps south of Oregon Inlet) · route/day scheduling · a read-only owner link
