# Can this pay rent? — the adversarial financial review
_2026-08-10. Ray asked directly: "will we make enough money doing this? I really need to pay rent." The agent was briefed NOT to be encouraging._

## THE ANSWER: not this winter.
**Realistic case, Ray's personal take Dec 2026 – Feb 2027 is roughly $4,000 TOTAL across all three months.**
The plan is structurally sound as a **2027–2028** business and structurally incapable of $7,000/month take-home in year one. **Bridge income through at least March 2027 is not optional.**

## 0. The mechanic that changes everything — only ~48¢ of a NET dollar reaches Ray
Net revenue × 60% labour pool × ~80% (his field + sales + admin share) = **~48% of NET, ~40–43% of GROSS.**
Every revenue figure in every research doc must be **nearly halved** before it means anything to rent.

**$7,000/mo take-home needs ~$14,600/mo NET ≈ $17,000/mo GROSS ≈ $200k+/year.**
Lifetime collections: **$4,452.** Customers ever: **4.** That is the distance.

## 4. ⚠️ THE STRATEGIC ERROR
$7k take-home ≈ $14,600 net/mo. At home watch's ~$86 effective hour that is **~170 billable hours/month** — beyond one man and a part-timer once drive time, sales, admin and junk runs are counted.
**The parked AV/Q-SYS line at $206/hr needs ~17 hours/week to produce the same number.**
> **He parked the only line whose unit economics can reach his own target.**

## 5. What has to change
1. **BRIDGE INCOME IS NOT OPTIONAL.** 15–20 hr/week at $55/hr = **$3,300–4,400/month — 2–3× what the business pays him all winter.** Winter field demand is only ~15–20 hr/week anyway, **so it does not compete with the business.** "Pride is the only obstacle; the model has no other solution."
2. **RESET THE TARGET.** $2,500–3,500/mo business take through 2027 **plus** bridge income = the household number.
3. **CUT THE RESERVE RATIOS TO SURVIVAL SETTINGS.** The 25% tax reserve is **nearly double the real liability** — MFJ, 3 kids, the Child Tax Credit wipes out federal income tax; real ≈ $8–9k vs ~$19–20k reserved. **15% with a quarterly true-up frees ~$800–900/month — the cheapest raise available.** Business fund to 5–7% frees ~$400 more.
4. **UNPARK AV/Q-SYS IN Q1 2027** once the winter recurring base is signed.

## 6. Highest-leverage 30 days: an all-out lights pre-sale, this week
- **Elasticity:** 5 vs 12 vs 22 jobs = **$7k vs $16.8k vs $31k** of season gross, decided almost entirely by Sept–Oct effort. A home-watch add moves $111/mo; a lights booking moves $1,400 now **plus a ~$1,200/yr renewal annuity at roughly double margin.**
- **Timing:** each booking = **$700 non-refundable in the account within days** — the only new-cash instrument inside the gap. **A week of delay in September cannot be bought back for twelve months.**
- **First calls:** property managers (−10% at 5+ homes turns **one relationship into 5–10 bookings** — the realistic-to-optimistic jump in a single deal) and Corolla oceanfront (no competitor serves Corolla, and it carries our zone surcharge).

## 7. What kills this, ranked
1. **The winter gap (near-certain without bridge income).** Likeliest death is not business failure — it is the household eating the tax reserve, the fund and the deposits by January, then a full-time job in February with obligations unmet.
2. **Home-watch acquisition stalls (high).** 1 add/month instead of 3 collapses 2027 to ~$1,200/mo.
3. **Mike Green (high, immediate).** 76% of lifetime revenue, slow-paying, newly price-resistant — and the junk baseline assumes his continued flow. **Any month's plan that depends on Mike Green paying that month is not a plan.**
4. **The November window (medium, severe).** 12–22 installs in ~4 weeks on ladders in coastal wind, deposits already spent. One nor'easter or one sprained ankle turns non-refundable deposits into refunds he cannot fund.
5. **Injury (medium, catastrophic).** One operator, no wage, no disability cover, three kids. Cheap accident/disability policy before install season; train Chase to run junk solo.
6. **Deposit float misuse (medium).**
7. **The $439/mo trailer note (corrosive).** Consumes most of the fund all winter, owed to **Chase — creditor and crew in one person**.

## 8. ⚠️ THE SKID STEER IS 2028
The fund, net of the trailer note and insurance, ends 2027 at ~$6,300. **$10k arrives mid-2028 at the earliest. Demolition and tree work are effectively not in this plan — stop counting them.**

## 9. My arithmetic — checked, and corrected
I said 45 homes + a lights season + junk + Starlink ≈ $145k gross ≈ "closer to $5,000/mo than $7,000."
**The arithmetic was right** ($145k → ~$4,815/mo). **Two corrections, both worse:**
1. **$145k is the OPTIMISTIC case, mislabelled as realistic.** It embeds 45 homes AND a 22-job season AND junk 60% above the demonstrated run-rate, all in year one. **Realistic 2027 gross is $96k → $3,173/month.**
2. **Ray's share is fragile.** If Chase does 40% of field hours instead of 25%, his pool share falls 80%→68% and the same $145k pays **$4,080/month.** The more the business grows, the more field hours leave his column.
