## PRESSURE & SOFT WASHING (the engine)

**Soft wash vs pressure wash — know the difference and lead with the right one:**
- **Soft wash** = low pressure (~100 PSI) + cleaning solution (SH/bleach + surfactant). **Use it on roofs, siding, stucco, screens, painted surfaces** — anything pressure would damage. It's what kills the **algae/mildew** the coast grows, and it lasts longer. ([Walsh Roofing](https://walshroofing.com/soft-wash-roof-cleaning-cost/))
- **Pressure wash** = high pressure. **Use it on concrete, driveways, sidewalks, dumpster pads, decks (carefully).** Never pressure-wash shingles — it strips the granules.

**Times (for your $/hr math):** a house soft wash runs **3–5 hours**; a driveway pressure wash **1–2 hours**. An experienced solo can knock out 4–8 small concrete jobs in a day.

**What's included in a wash quote:** pre-rinse + plant protection, solution application + dwell, rinse, spot-treat stains, basic cleanup. **Not included** (quote separately): sealing/staining, gutter brightening, screen removal, heavy oil stains.

**Sizing house washes:** 1-story ≤1,500 sqft → $299; ≤2,500 → $399; 2-story 2,500–3,500 → $549; bigger → quote. Roof soft wash from $399. Driveway by car-count (small/med/large $99/$149/$199).

**Sell recurring.** Salt + humidity bring algae back **every year** — so every wash is a recurring-plan candidate (20% off recurring). That's the whole margin game.

## HOUSE-WATCH / PROPERTY CHECKS (the wedge)

What it is: scheduled visits to **absentee-owned homes** (60% of OBX) — walk the property, check for leaks/pests/storm damage/break-ins, run water, flush toilets, confirm HVAC, and send a **photo/text report.** Insurers often **require** checks on vacant homes.

**What's included per visit:** interior + exterior walk, photo report, basic checks (water, HVAC, doors/windows, pests, storm damage). **Add-ons:** package/mail handling, contractor meet-ins, pre/post-storm prep, turning utilities on/off for guests.

**Pricing:** monthly visit $50; bi-weekly $45/visit; weekly $40/visit; one-off $75; storm check $95. A visit is ~30–45 min including drive — **near-zero materials, high margin, recurring, sticky.** ([Home-watch pricing](https://www.personalpropertymanagers.com/absentee-home-watch.php))

**Why it matters most:** once you're the trusted person at the property, you get the washing, junk, gutters, *and* the Jamieson tech work. It's the cheapest customer-acquisition you have.

## JUNK REMOVAL

By volume (truck-bed fraction) or job: average **$350–850**. Quote on site by load size + dump fees (~$62/ton). Needs the **trailer** (gating item). Pairs with estate/rental clean-outs for the same PM/absentee customers. Margin 40–72% solo. ([Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/academy/junk-removal/how-to-price-a-junk-removal-job/))

## WINDOWS & GUTTERS (bundle, don't lead)

- **Windows:** $12/window exterior, $18 int+ext, +$4/window 2nd floor; storefronts from $99. ~$150–370/residential visit.
- **Gutters:** from $149, or $1.50/linear ft; gutter-guard install = quote. Needed ~twice a year.
- These are saturated as standalone — **attach them to wash/house-watch accounts** as recurring add-ons (near-zero acquisition cost).

## PARKING-LOT & COMMERCIAL CLEANUP

Litter/debris pickup for lots, storefronts, dumpster areas, roadsides. **Quote by lot size — use the Map tool** to draw the lot and estimate parking spaces, then price by space count. Recurring (daily/weekly) is the goal; offer 20% off recurring. Forgiving schedule = good fit for a part-time crew.
