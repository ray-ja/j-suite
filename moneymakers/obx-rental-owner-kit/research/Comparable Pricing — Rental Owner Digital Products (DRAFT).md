# Comparable Pricing — Rental-Owner Digital Products

*Quick market pull to price the OBX Rental Owner's Operations Kit. What comparable digital products for short-term-rental owners actually sell for, by tier, with a positioning recommendation. Draft — directional; spot-check live listings before locking a price.*

---

## What the market charges (3 tiers)

**Tier 1 — Single templates / cheap downloads: ~$7–12**
Individual Canva welcome books, one-off spreadsheets, single checklists. Etsy is flooded with these (e.g., welcome-book templates ~$6.60, Airbnb/investment spreadsheets ~$7.41–$8.32). Commodity pricing, race to the bottom, near-zero differentiation.

**Tier 2 — Multi-item bundles: ~$23–32**
The sweet spot for a *kit*. Bundles that combine welcome book + cleaning checklist + signage + trackers (e.g., Airbnb host bundle ~$24.16; STR property tracker ~$23.83; property-spreadsheet bundles ~$31.27). This is where a curated, multi-section "operations kit" naturally sits.

**Tier 3 — Courses / premium toolkits: ~$97–$497 (full courses $500–$2,000+)**
Structured programs with templates + video/coaching + certification. $97 and $197 are the classic anchor price points in STR education. A kit can reach toward the bottom of this tier *if* it bundles enough perceived value and a credible local angle.

---

## What makes ours worth more than a $24 Etsy bundle

Generic bundles are national and interchangeable. Ours has three things they can't copy:

1. **Hyper-local — OBX-specific.** Storm prep, salt-air/humidity, hurricane-season empty-home risk, local home-watch/cleanup tie-ins. An owner here will pay more for "built for the Outer Banks" than for a generic template.
2. **The tech-ready section is genuinely operational**, not decorative — a real readiness audit with the "why," not just a pretty welcome book.
3. **A real local services backstop.** The kit isn't just paper; it connects to actual Jamieson (tech) and OBX Lot Solutions (cleanup/home-watch) services. That makes it a front door to high-value work, which changes the pricing math entirely (see below).

---

## Recommendation

**Price the kit at $39–$49** as a standalone digital product — a justified premium over the $23–32 generic bundles on the strength of the OBX-specific, operationally-real positioning, while staying an easy impulse buy for an owner managing a property worth hundreds of thousands.

Then consider a **two-tier offer:**

- **Kit (digital) — $39–49.** The download.
- **Kit + free tech-readiness assessment / consult — same price, lead-gen framing.** The kit *is* the lead magnet for Jamieson + OBX Lot Solutions. Even at $0–49, if 1 in 20 buyers converts to a rental-tech package (~$1,300–6,200) or a recurring home-watch/cleanup contract, the kit's real value is the **funnel**, not the unit price.

**Strategic read:** don't over-optimize the sticker. The kit's job is twofold — make a little margin as a product *and* feed the two service businesses. Priced at $39–49 it clears the "this is substantive" bar without friction, and every buyer becomes a warm lead for far larger recurring revenue. **The kit is customer-acquisition that pays for itself.**

A free or $0 "lite" version (just the tech-ready checklist) as a pure lead magnet is also worth testing — it maximizes top-of-funnel for the service businesses.
