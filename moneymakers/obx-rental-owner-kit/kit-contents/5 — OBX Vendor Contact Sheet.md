## ★ OBX vendor tips (the local reality)
- **Book the busy-season standing slots early.** Good cleaners and pool/hot-tub services fill up by spring — lock weekly slots before May.
- **Always have a backup.** Weekend turnover demand means your primary will sometimes be unavailable exactly when you need them.
- **Vet for reliability over price.** In a market short on dependable labor, the cheap vendor who no-shows costs you a guest refund and a bad review.
- **One local who does multiple things is gold** — washing + junk + checks under one number cuts your vendor list and your stress.
- **Keep this sheet current** — re-verify numbers each spring before the season.

---

*This sheet is most valuable when your "home-watch," "washing," "junk," and "tech" rows point to people you trust. If you don't have those locked yet, that's exactly the gap a reliable local property-care company fills.*
