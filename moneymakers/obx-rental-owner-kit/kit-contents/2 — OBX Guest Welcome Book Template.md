# The OBX Guest Welcome Book — Fill-in Template

*Part of the OBX Rental Owner's Operations Kit. A guest welcome book that cuts your "how does the X work?" messages, earns better reviews, and makes guests feel like locals. Fill in the [bracketed] fields, print it, and leave a copy in the home (and as a PDF in your listing). Written for Outer Banks rentals specifically.*

> **Why this matters:** the #1 driver of effortless 5-star reviews is guests who never had to ask a question. A good welcome book is the cheapest review insurance you'll ever buy.
