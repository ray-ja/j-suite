# The OBX Seasonal Maintenance Calendar

*Part of the OBX Rental Owner's Operations Kit. A month-by-month maintenance rhythm for an Outer Banks rental — built around the coast's real cycle: booked-solid summers, hurricane season, and the salt-air wear that never stops. Follow it and you trade expensive surprises for small, scheduled tasks.*

> **How to use:** Set calendar reminders for each block. Tasks marked ★ are coastal-specific. "DIY or pro" notes where it's worth hiring out. If your home is far away, this doubles as the scope you hand a local property-care team.

---

## The guiding idea
A coastal rental takes more wear than a normal home *and* earns most of its money in a short window. So: **fix and prep in the shoulder seasons, protect during the booked season, button up for winter.** Salt, humidity, sun, and storms are the four forces — every task below fights one of them.

---

## WINTER — January / February (slow season: deep maintenance)
*Bookings are light; this is your repair-and-upgrade window.*
- [ ] ★ Full interior deep clean + inspect for off-season humidity/mold
- [ ] Service/replace HVAC filters; schedule a pro HVAC tune-up before summer demand
- [ ] ★ Check for freeze risk — keep heat ≥ 55°F or winterize lines if vacant
- [ ] Inspect roof, flashing, and seals from the storm season
- [ ] Touch-up paint, caulk, and any wood rot (salt + moisture accelerate it)
- [ ] Deep-clean or replace tired linens, towels, kitchenware
- [ ] Review last year's reviews — fix the recurring complaints now
- [ ] ★ Re-seal decks/exterior wood if due (coastal sun destroys finishes)

## EARLY SPRING — March (get ready to earn)
- [ ] ★ Pressure/soft wash exterior — winter salt + algae buildup *(pro)*
- [ ] Wash windows + check screens (salt film, tears) *(pro or DIY)*
- [ ] Service the grill; replace propane
- [ ] Test all appliances, TVs, wifi, smart locks before peak
- [ ] Inspect + clean outdoor furniture; replace faded cushions
- [ ] ★ Hot tub/pool: open, service, balance chemicals *(pro)*
- [ ] Restock all consumables to full
- [ ] Pest control treatment before the season *(pro)*

## SPRING — April / May (final prep + early bookings)
- [ ] Gutter clean-out + check drainage *(pro)*
- [ ] ★ Check AC is fully ready — first hot week sells out fast and AC failure = refunds
- [ ] Replace HVAC filters; confirm thermostat schedule
- [ ] Deep clean + staging refresh; new listing photos if anything changed
- [ ] Test smoke/CO detectors; replace batteries
- [ ] Confirm cleaner + vendor schedule locked for summer (see Vendor Sheet)
- [ ] ★ Stock hurricane/storm supplies before season (see Storm-Prep Plan)

## SUMMER — June / July / August (booked solid: protect, don't disrupt)
*Minimal invasive work — keep the machine running.*
- [ ] ★ Hurricane season is live (Jun 1–Nov 30) — keep the Storm-Prep Plan ready
- [ ] Weekly: HVAC filter check in heavy use; confirm AC performance
- [ ] Hot tub/pool: regular service cadence *(pro/weekly)*
- [ ] Between turns: quick maintenance sweeps (see Turnover Checklist)
- [ ] Monitor reviews in real time; fix issues same-week
- [ ] ★ Keep an eye on the tropics; pre-position storm prep when a system forms
- [ ] Mid-season exterior rinse if salt/algae returns *(pro)*

## EARLY FALL — September / October (peak storm risk + shoulder bookings)
- [ ] ★ Peak hurricane months — Storm-Prep Plan on standby, supplies stocked
- [ ] Post-storm inspections as needed (document everything)
- [ ] HVAC: switch-season check
- [ ] Gutter clean after any storms; clear debris
- [ ] ★ Deck/exterior wash to clear summer salt + sunscreen grime *(pro)*
- [ ] Begin booking winter repair projects with vendors

## LATE FALL / EARLY WINTER — November / December (wind down + protect)
- [ ] ★ Winterize if going vacant: heat ≥ 55°F or drain lines; protect pipes
- [ ] Hot tub/pool: winter service or close *(pro)*
- [ ] Final gutter clean before winter
- [ ] Bring in/secure outdoor furniture + beach gear for the season
- [ ] ★ Check all seals, weatherstripping, storm shutters before winter storms
- [ ] Deep clean; assess what needs replacing in the Jan/Feb window
- [ ] Holiday booking? Confirm heat, safety, welcome book current

---

## Recurring (year-round, regardless of month)
- HVAC filters: every 1–3 months (more in heavy summer use)
- Smoke/CO detectors: test quarterly, batteries 2×/year
- Pest monitoring: ongoing
- ★ Exterior salt/algae: watch and wash as needed — the coast never stops
- Review every guest review for maintenance signals
