# The OBX Turnover & Cleaning Checklist

*Part of the OBX Rental Owner's Operations Kit. A room-by-room turnover system built for Outer Banks vacation rentals — same-day weekend turns, salt-and-sand reality, and the details that protect your 5-star reviews. Print one per turn, or load it into your cleaner's app.*

> **How to use:** Hand this to your cleaning team and walk it once together. The goal is a turn that's repeatable by anyone, so the home is guest-ready whether your A-team or a fill-in does it. Items marked ★ are the OBX-specific ones most checklists miss.

---

## Before you start (5 minutes)
- [ ] Confirm checkout happened / guest has left
- [ ] Note the next check-in time — back-to-back same-day turn? Prioritize accordingly
- [ ] Photograph any damage or items left behind **before** touching anything (protects your deposit claim)
- [ ] Open windows to air out (★ coastal homes hold humidity and odors fast)
- [ ] Start a laundry load first thing so it runs while you clean

---

## Kitchen
- [ ] Dishes washed/put away; dishwasher run and emptied
- [ ] Counters, backsplash, stovetop degreased
- [ ] Inside microwave, oven front, toaster crumbs
- [ ] ★ Fridge emptied of guest leftovers; wiped; check freezer for ice buildup
- [ ] Coffee maker rinsed; restock filters, coffee, sugar, creamer pods
- [ ] Trash + recycling out; fresh liners; ★ confirm bins secured (raccoons/wind)
- [ ] Restock: paper towels, dish soap, sponge, trash bags, dishwasher pods
- [ ] Inventory count: glasses, mugs, plates, silverware, cookware (note shortfalls)

## Bathrooms (repeat per bath)
- [ ] Toilet scrubbed inside + base + behind
- [ ] Shower/tub scrubbed; ★ check for sand in drains and corners
- [ ] Mirror, counter, sink, fixtures polished
- [ ] Floor washed
- [ ] Fresh towels (set per max occupancy + spares), hand towels, washcloths, bath mat
- [ ] Restock: toilet paper (2+ rolls/bath), hand soap, shampoo/conditioner/body wash, tissues
- [ ] Empty trash; fresh liner

## Bedrooms
- [ ] Beds stripped, fresh linens, beds made hotel-style
- [ ] ★ Check under beds + nightstands for guest items / sand
- [ ] Dust surfaces, lamps, fans, headboards
- [ ] Closet: hangers counted, extra blanket + pillows present
- [ ] Windows/blinds wiped; ★ check screens for salt film and tears
- [ ] Floors vacuumed/mopped
- [ ] Trash emptied

## Living areas
- [ ] Sofas/chairs vacuumed incl. under cushions (remotes, crumbs, sand)
- [ ] Surfaces, TV, electronics dusted; remotes wiped + working (fresh batteries on hand)
- [ ] Confirm wifi works + the network/password card is displayed
- [ ] Floors vacuumed/mopped
- [ ] Throw pillows/blankets straightened; décor reset to staging photos
- [ ] ★ Sliding-door tracks cleared of sand; door glass cleaned

## ★ OBX-specific exterior & extras
- [ ] Sweep sand off porches, decks, stairs, entry mats
- [ ] Outdoor furniture wiped + arranged; cushions present and dry
- [ ] Grill cleaned + propane checked (★ a dead tank is a common bad review)
- [ ] Rinse station/outdoor shower clear and working
- [ ] Beach gear inventoried (chairs, umbrella, cart, boogie boards) + sand rinsed
- [ ] Hot tub (if applicable): chemicals checked, cover secured
- [ ] Pool area (if applicable): skimmed, deck swept, gate latched
- [ ] Bikes/kayaks accounted for and secured
- [ ] Exterior trash/recycling bins staged for pickup day

## Whole-home final pass
- [ ] HVAC set to owner default (★ don't leave it cold/off in summer — mold; or off in a freeze)
- [ ] All lights off, doors + windows locked
- [ ] Smart lock: ★ confirm prior guest's code is expired and next guest's code is set
- [ ] Smoke/CO detectors present and not chirping
- [ ] Welcome book + house rules displayed
- [ ] Welcome touch in place (see Guest Welcome Book) — small thing, big review impact
- [ ] Final walk-through against the staging photos: does it look like the listing?
- [ ] Photograph the finished home (proof of condition + fresh listing content)

---

## Restock master list (keep this stocked)
**Paper/cleaning:** paper towels, toilet paper, tissues, trash bags, dish soap, dishwasher pods, all-purpose cleaner, sponges.
**Bath:** hand soap, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, makeup wipes.
**Kitchen:** coffee + filters, sugar, creamer, salt/pepper, dish sponge, foil/wrap, paper plates (optional).
**Safety/utility:** batteries (AA/AAA/9V), light bulbs, fire extinguisher charged, first-aid basics.

## Turn-day timing tips (★ OBX weekend reality)
- Friday/Saturday/Sunday turns stack up — book your cleaner standing weekly, don't scramble.
- A 3–5 bedroom OBX home is a **3–5 hour** turn for a team of two. Budget it.
- Sand and salt add ~20% to a coastal turn vs. inland — plan extra time for decks, tracks, and beach gear.
