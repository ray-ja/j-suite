# The OBX Rental Owner's Operations Kit — Overview & Production Notes

*The product, its contents, how it makes money, and what's left to ship it. **Draft — nothing sold or published until Ray approves.***

---

## What this is
A **sellable digital product** (3-tier: **$29 / $39 / $49**, $19 launch promo) for Outer Banks vacation-rental owners and property managers: a polished, OBX-specific bundle of the six core operational templates every rental needs, plus a premium-tier tech-ready guide. Zero ongoing upkeep once built. It does triple duty:

1. **Direct revenue** — sold as a one-time digital download (Gumroad/Payhip/Stripe link, or on the OBX Lot Solutions / Second-Home Hub sites).
2. **Lead magnet / list builder** — a "lite" version (or the full kit) captures emails that feed our sequences.
3. **Cross-sell funnel** — every component naturally points to OBX Lot Solutions (home-watch, washing, junk) and Jamieson Automation (smart monitoring, locks, wifi). The kit *is* a sales tool that customers pay us to receive.

## The components (in `kit-contents/`)
1. **OBX Turnover & Cleaning Checklist** — room-by-room, coastal-specific, restock + timing.
2. **OBX Guest Welcome Book Template** — fill-in template + local-guide section; cuts guest messages, lifts reviews.
3. **OBX Seasonal Maintenance Calendar** — month-by-month, built on the coast's cycle + hurricane season.
4. **OBX Storm-Prep Plan** — before/during/after hurricane plan + guest comms template.
5. **OBX Vendor Contact Sheet** — fill-in bench with primary/backup, utilities, emergency.
6. **OBX Pricing & Seasonality Cheat Sheet** — the demand curve + a 4-step pricing framework.
7. **OBX Tech-Ready Rental Setup Guide** *(premium-tier bonus)* — the rental tech stack + DIY-vs-pro + self-audit; the bridge into Jamieson installs. *(From Jamieson Ops's outline, written into kit voice.)*

## Why owners will pay for it
It's **OBX-specific**, not generic. Salt/sand turns, Sat–Sat summer weeks, sound-side sunsets, county evacuation reality, hurricane-season operations, occupancy-tax reminders — the details only a local would know. A new owner saves themselves weeks of trial-and-error and a few bad reviews for the price of a dinner. That specificity is the moat and the justification for a paid (not free) product.

## The honest cross-sell (built into every piece)
Each component soft-points to our services without being an ad — because the advice is genuinely good and we genuinely do the work:
- Turnover/Vendor → reliable cleaning, washing, junk (OBX Lot Solutions)
- Maintenance/Storm → home-watch + property checks (OBX Lot Solutions)
- Storm/Welcome → smart monitoring, locks, wifi, Starlink (Jamieson Automation)
The kit earns trust by being useful first; the services are the natural next step for anyone who'd rather not DIY.
