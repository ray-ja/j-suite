Why Nags Head second homes need a local watch

  • Iconic oceanfront cottages take direct salt, wind, and surge.
  • Many homes are seasonal with out-of-state owners.
  • Sand, humidity, and storms quietly damage unwatched houses.

From Jockey's Ridge and the historic Nags Head cottage row to South Nags Head, we keep watch across the town.

💡 Out of state? That's exactly who we're for. We're your local eyes in Nags Head — we check the home, catch problems early, and send a photo report you can read from anywhere.

What every Nags Head home-watch visit includes

  • Exterior walk: roof (from ground), gutters, siding, windows, deck, crawlspace/pilings
  • Interior walk: every room, ceilings & walls for stains, under sinks, water heater & HVAC
  • Run faucets, check toilets, confirm no leaks, smells, or pests
  • Verify HVAC/humidity and that no breakers tripped
  • Pre- and post-storm checks during hurricane season
  • A dated photo report after every visit
