Why it matters here

The Outer Banks is hard on empty houses

This isn't a normal market. Homes face constant salt spray, wind-driven rain, sand, and humidity — then sit unoccupied for the off-season. Small failures don't get noticed: a slow plumbing leak, a tripped HVAC, a window left cracked before a nor'easter, a mini-split dripping into a wall.

  • Water is the #1 enemy — leaks and humidity cause mold and rot long before you visit again.
  • Storms don't wait for your schedule — damage compounds when no one checks for weeks.
  • Insurers are tightening up — many policies require documented checks on vacant homes.
  • Distance = blind spots — you can't smell a problem or hear a sump pump from out of state.
