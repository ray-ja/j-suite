Why Manteo second homes need a local watch

  • Waterfront and soundside homes face humidity and flooding risk.
  • Second homes and investment properties sit empty between stays.
  • Historic and older homes need a careful, regular eye.

From downtown Manteo and the waterfront to Roanoke Island Festival Park and Pirate's Cove, we serve the island.

💡 Out of state? That's exactly who we're for. We're your local eyes in Manteo — we check the home, catch problems early, and send a photo report you can read from anywhere.

What every Manteo home-watch visit includes

  • Exterior walk: roof (from ground), gutters, siding, windows, deck, crawlspace/pilings
  • Interior walk: every room, ceilings & walls for stains, under sinks, water heater & HVAC
  • Run faucets, check toilets, confirm no leaks, smells, or pests
  • Verify HVAC/humidity and that no breakers tripped
  • Pre- and post-storm checks during hurricane season
  • A dated photo report after every visit
