Gear we'd put in our own clients' homes

A short, opinionated list — not a catalog. These are the devices that prevent the most expensive failures in an empty coastal home, in priority order.

1 · Water-leak sensors (do this first)

💧
Highest ROI

Wi-Fi leak sensor (3–6 pack)

Place at the water heater, under sinks, behind toilets, and in the HVAC drain pan. Phone alert the moment it's wet. The cheapest disaster-prevention you can buy.

🚰
Best protection

Automatic water-shutoff valve

Detects a leak or freeze and shuts the main automatically — the closest thing to insurance against a burst line in a vacant home. Worth professional install.

2 · Climate: freeze & humidity

🌡️
Off-season

Smart thermostat

Hold a safe temperature and get alerts if the home drifts toward a freeze. Control from anywhere.

Mold defense

Temp / humidity sensor

Catch a dead HVAC or rising dampness early; keep RH in the 45–55% safe zone.

3 · Smart locks & access

🔒
Owner favorite

Smart lock with keypad

Codes for cleaners, contractors, and your home-watch service — control and revoke from your phone, log every entry. No hidden key.

4 · Cameras

🎥
Eyes on entries

Video doorbell + exterior cam

Deterrent + a live look at the property. Battery/Wi-Fi cams are easy DIY; wired PoE is more reliable for a vacant home.

Storm-ready
