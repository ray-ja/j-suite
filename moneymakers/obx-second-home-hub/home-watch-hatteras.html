Why Hatteras second homes need a local watch

  • Extremely remote — hours from most owners and prone to storm isolation and overwash.
  • Marine air and flooding work hard on empty homes.
  • When NC-12 closes, only a local can check on the house.

From Hatteras village and the harbor to the Cape Hatteras Lighthouse area and the ferry docks, we cover the south end of the island.

💡 Out of state? That's exactly who we're for. We're your local eyes in Hatteras — we check the home, catch problems early, and send a photo report you can read from anywhere.

What every Hatteras home-watch visit includes

  • Exterior walk: roof (from ground), gutters, siding, windows, deck, crawlspace/pilings
  • Interior walk: every room, ceilings & walls for stains, under sinks, water heater & HVAC
  • Run faucets, check toilets, confirm no leaks, smells, or pests
  • Verify HVAC/humidity and that no breakers tripped
  • Pre- and post-storm checks during hurricane season
  • A dated photo report after every visit
