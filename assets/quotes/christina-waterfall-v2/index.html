About the water

The tile that sits over the water, the basin covers and the top band, goes over a waterproof membrane with silicone at the glass. That is how tile over water gets built, so it is in the price, not an extra.

Roman clay is not a wet-area finish, and you know the waterfall better than we do. So on day one we run it for ten minutes and mark where the water lands on each side. If it stays in the basin, nothing more is needed. If it reaches the clay returns, a wipeable topcoat on the returns is $120, added only then and only after you have seen the test.

About 10 working days on site, water off for 6 of them. The 50% deposit holds your dates and covers the tile and Roman clay order. The balance is due when the whole job is finished, columns and niches included.
