## Two things to keep clean

**Licensing line.** You're not a licensed electrician, so keep Tier 1 to low-voltage / no-mains work — Starlink, networking, battery smart locks, low-voltage thermostats, wireless cameras, TV mounts. Anything that requires tapping line voltage, a hardwired transformer, or panel work should be subbed to a licensed electrician or declined. Protects you and the brand.

**Don't let the brands bleed.** Skilled tech stays under Jamieson (premium pricing + you own 100%). Cleanup/wash stays under OBX Lot Solutions. The shared win is the *relationship*: a property manager who books a power wash is a warm lead for a Starlink or camera job — cross-refer, don't merge.
