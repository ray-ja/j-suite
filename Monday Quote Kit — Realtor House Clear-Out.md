# Monday Quote Kit — Realtor House Clear-Out

**The visit:** Monday, in-person quote. **The job:** June 17 — clear out everything the new owner doesn't want from a house the realtor just sold. **Your real goal Monday isn't the job — it's the realtor.** She sells houses all year, and every sale = a clear-out, often a clean, sometimes a house-watch. Land this and look pro, and she becomes a referral pipe. Play the long game.

---

## Before you walk in
- You're the calm expert who's done this a hundred times. You have. Quote with confidence — blind pricing is what we killed with the cost model.
- Bring: phone w/ the J-Suite app (Guided Quote), tape measure, **business cards**, the leave-behind flyer. Take **photos of every room** (you'll size the load from them and protect yourself on scope).
- Get her **name, cell, email, brokerage** before you leave. She's going in your Accounts as a referral source.

## On-site walkthrough — assess these
- **Every space:** bedrooms, living, kitchen, garage, attic/crawlspace, shed, porch, outside piles. Garages and sheds hide the volume.
- **Heavy/special items** (these add cost — note them): appliances, mattresses, furniture, TVs/e-waste, tires, pianos/safes.
- **Stuff the C&D landfill WON'T take** — paint, chemicals, propane, fuel, hazardous. Don't promise to haul these; they need separate handling. Flag them to the realtor.
- **Access:** stairs, distance to where the dumpster/truck sits, parking, narrow halls. Bad access = more labor.
- **Ask:** "Is anything here already promised to someone, or do you want donatable items dropped at a charity?" (Diverting good stuff cuts your dump weight and earns goodwill — and a donation receipt for the seller is a nice touch.)

## How to size it (volume = truckloads)
Junk is priced by **volume**, not weight. A packed house is typically **2–4 full truckloads**.
- Eyeball each room as a fraction of a load; sum them. A medium 3-bed house lightly furnished ≈ **1.5–2 loads**; heavily packed ≈ **3–4**.
- **2 full loads ≈ one 20-yard dumpster.** 3–4 loads ≈ 30-yard.

## How to price it — two paths (the app does the math live)

**▶ Option A — roll-off dumpster (recommended; you have no trailer).** The dumpster price already includes hauling + tonnage, so you skip the Manns Harbor runs.
- 20-yd ≈ **$300–450/wk**, 30-yd ≈ **$350–500/wk** (NC). Confirm a local quote from **Soundside Recycling** (services Dare) before the 17th.
- **Your cost** = dumpster + crew labor + a little gas. **Pass the dumpster through** as a line item, then your labor + margin.

**▶ Option B — self-haul in the pickup.** Only if it's a small load.
- Disposal: **$73.16/ton** at the C&D landfill (first 500 lbs free), plus gas and *many* trips. For a whole house this eats your day — Option A wins.

**Worked example (medium 3-bed, ~2 loads, Option A):**
- 20-yd dumpster ~$450 + two brothers ~14 crew-hrs × $18 = ~$250 + gas ~$30 → **true cost ≈ $730.**
- Market for a 3-bed cleanout: **$1,000–$2,500** (national avg ~$1,250; full truck $700–1,000 each).
- Quote **~$1,600–1,900** → **~$900–1,150 profit**, brothers paid, dumpster covered. That's your floor visibility.

## What to actually quote
- 3-bed house: **$1,000–$2,500** depending on volume/access. Large 2,000+ sq ft or packed: **$3,000–5,500**.
- **Don't underprice a whole house** to "win it" — you'll work a full day for scraps. Price the volume you saw.
- Give her a **written quote same-day** from the app (print/PDF). A firm number on paper beats a verbal "around a thousand."

## The close + the referral ask
1. Present the written quote, confirm the June 17 date, note the dumpster gets dropped a day prior.
2. Then plant the pipe: *"I also handle pressure washing, and home-watch checks on vacant listings — if that's ever useful for a sale, I'm your one call."*
3. Ask directly: *"I'd love to be your go-to for clear-outs on your future sales."* Realtors refer the people who make them look good.

## Logistics before the 17th
- Book the roll-off with Soundside once the quote's accepted (confirm drop date).
- Lock both brothers for the 17th; whole-house = a full crew day.
- Stripe payment link ready so she can pay on completion (being set up).
