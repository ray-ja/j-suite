## On every call (from the script)
- **Opener:** "Hi, this is Ray with OBX Lot Solutions — we do exterior soft-washing and property checks for vacation-rental and absentee-owned homes. Are you the right person to talk to about keeping your managed homes clean and looked-after?"
- **Ask for** the maintenance / property-services coordinator (the real decision-maker).
- **The ask:** "What day works — Wednesday or Thursday? ... I'll be there in uniform, and you'll have a written quote the same day." Get **address + cell/email.**
- **Recurring = 20% off.** Always offer it — recurring is the whole point.
- **Don't lead with price.** Anchors only if pushed (watch $40–50/visit recurring · soft-wash house $299–549 · roof from $399).
