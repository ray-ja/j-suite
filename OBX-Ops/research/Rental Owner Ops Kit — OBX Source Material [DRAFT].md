*Research for Marketing. The OBX-specific facts that make the Kit credible and worth paying for. Draft, cited. May 2026.*

## 1 · Turnover & cleaning standards (the credibility core)

- **Turn day is Saturday** in peak season — most OBX homes run **Saturday-to-Saturday weekly** rentals; some managers also offer Friday/Sunday check-ins (e.g., Outer Banks Blue uses Fri/Sat/Sun). **Check-in access is typically 4–6 PM**, so the crew has a tight same-day window to flip the house.
- A full turnover clean runs **~3–5 hours** depending on size/condition and includes: **linen change, kitchen + baths, bedrooms, living areas, trash-out, restock consumables, high-touch sanitizing, damage/missing-item check, and a final inspection.**
- **Coastal-specific turn tasks** that generic checklists miss: **sand in tile grout, window tracks, and carpet; salt residue on glass and fixtures; bathroom/closet moisture and mildew.** These are the details an OBX owner recognizes as "this person actually knows our houses."
- Standard pro practice: home is cleaned **after every departure**, and guests are told to report any miss **immediately on arrival** — so a documented checklist + inspection protects the owner from review damage.
- Managers increasingly **launder linens in-house** for consistency and on-time turn delivery (KEES does this; standard linen package = fitted/flat sheets + pillowcases per bed, plus bath towels, hand towels, washcloths, bath mats per bath).

**Kit angle:** a printable **OBX turn-day checklist** (with the coastal tasks) + a linen-par-level worksheet + a "what to tell your cleaner" standard.

## 2 · Seasonality & rate patterns (so owners price right)

- **Peak: Memorial Day → Labor Day**, a bell-curve with the **highest weekly rates in July**; oceanfront books months ahead. Weekly rentals dominate summer.
- **Shoulder: April–May and September–October** (some include March & November). Warm, less crowded, **rates noticeably lower** — spring can run **~half of in-season** for the same home.
- **Off-season: late fall → early spring** — lowest rates, more flexibility, more short stays than weeks.
- Managers price in **tiers** — typically *prime / in / mid / off* season rates. Illustrative annual shape from the sources: **~12 peak weeks** at top rate, **~10 shoulder weeks** mid, **~5 off weeks** low (e.g., $3,500 / $1,500 / $1,000 per week respectively in one worked example).
- **Check-in Saturdays carry the heaviest island traffic** — relevant for scheduling turns, deliveries, and any owner visits.

**Kit angle:** an **OBX rate-calendar template** (tag each week prime/in/mid/off) and a "don't leave shoulder weeks empty" pricing nudge.

## 4 · Hurricane / storm specifics for second homes (the OBX trust signal)

- **Dare County reentry permits.** After a mandatory evacuation, the island reopens via a **4-priority staged reentry**. **Non-resident property owners are Priority 3** and get back in with a **current real-property tax bill or property record + matching photo ID, or a reentry permit + ID.** Permits are **free, good for the calendar year, and prior-year permits are NOT accepted** — apply yearly at **DareNC.gov/Reentry**. (Currituck/Corolla has its own process.)
- **Evacuation → possible tenant refunds.** Under NC law, a mandatory evacuation can obligate an owner to **refund guests**; standard homeowner policies often **don't** cover that lost income — owners need **"loss of business income"/specialized STR coverage.**
- **Flood insurance reality.** Homes in FEMA **Zone A/V with a mortgage must carry flood insurance**; OBX (Dare/Currituck barrier islands) is among the **highest-cost** areas — **Zone V coastal $2,000–$5,000+/yr** vs. NC average ~$916. Wind/hail is often a **separate** policy/deductible. Owners should confirm coverage *before* hurricane season (Jun 1 – Nov 30).
- **Pre-storm prep checklist** (board-up/shutters, bring in furniture, kill power to non-essentials, document condition with photos) and **post-storm inspection** are recurring, premium services — ties directly to our storm-check ($95) offering.

**Kit angle:** an **"OBX Hurricane Playbook for Absentee Owners"** — reentry-permit how-to, an insurance-coverage checklist, and a pre/post-storm task list. This is the single most credibility-building, locally-specific piece in the Kit.
