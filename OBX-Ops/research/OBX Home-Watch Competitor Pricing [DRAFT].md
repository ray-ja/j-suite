## Named direct competitors (dedicated home-watch, not VRMs)

### 1. OBX First Watch — *the only one publishing prices*
- Contact: (252) 256-9617 · obxfirstwatch.com · based Kill Devil Hills
- **Pricing — per interior+exterior inspection, by zone (distance-based):**
  - **$65** — Southern Shores, Kitty Hawk, Kill Devil Hills, Nags Head
  - **$75** — S. Nags Head (Jeannette's Pier) to S. Oregon Inlet Rd., Manteo, Duck, Pine Island, Corolla
  - **$85** — third/remote zone (farthest reaches)
  - Rates assume **4–8 bedroom** homes; 9+ BR quoted separately.
- Model: priced per inspection (ad hoc / scheduled), rate set by drive time + distance from KDH.

### 2. Home Check OBX
- Contact: (252) 207-5949 · homecheckobx.com · Kill Devil Hills (PO Box)
- Plans: **Weekly, Bi-Monthly, Monthly, Storm, and One-Time** home checks. Detailed emailed report after each. Requires key/code access.
- Edge: **20+ years construction background** ("better eye for detail"); avg rating cited 9.6. Serves Corolla → Hatteras.
- **Pricing: not published — contact for quote.**

### 3. Cottage Comforts (outerbankshousewatch.com)
- Based Kitty Hawk (operator: Steve Keller) · serves Southern Shores → Corolla
- Plans: **weekly year-round**, **winter-season weekly**, **summer monthly** checks of rental homes, and **short-term (30-day min)** for locals away. Weekly **email reports**.
- Also sells concierge **homeowner services**: changing air filters, accepting deliveries, obtaining estimates for lawn care / painting / **pressure washing** / window cleaning, beach-vehicle service. Aligns with NHWA standards.
- **Pricing: not published — contact for quote.**

### Also in the mix
- **Vacation-rental managers** (Twiddy, Resort, Seaside Vacations, etc. — see Top 20 list) bundle owner/"home check" services for their managed homes; they're an indirect substitute for owners already in a rental program.
- **National Home Watch Association** runs an accredited-member directory (nationalhomewatchassociation.org) — useful for spotting any new accredited entrant and for the credibility angle (accreditation as a trust signal).

## What this means for us *(positioning)*
- **Market anchor:** the only public per-inspection price on OBX is **$65–$85**. Our Service Guide recurring rates are **$40 (weekly) / $45 (bi-weekly) / $50 (monthly) per visit**, plus **$75 one-off** and **$95 storm**. Our recurring per-visit pricing sits **below** the competitor's per-inspection anchor — read as strong value, but see the flag below.
- **Differentiators to lean on:** a **branded photo report every visit** (competitors send text/email reports — photos beat prose); **reliability** ("shows up"); and **bundling** — washing, junk, gutters, and Jamieson tech that pure home-watch shops can't offer.
- **Their edge to neutralize:** Home Check OBX's construction background; Cottage Comforts' concierge menu (we already match most of it).
