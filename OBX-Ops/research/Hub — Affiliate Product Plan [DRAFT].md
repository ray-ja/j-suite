# OBX Second-Home Care Hub — Affiliate Product Plan
*Research for Marketing & Dev. Named products, real prices, affiliate economics. Draft, cited. May 2026.*

Each category maps to a pain point in the Audience & SEO Brief and to a buying-guide post. Lead with the problem ("come back to a flooded house"), then the gear, then a CTA to our house-watch service (the gear watches; we verify in person).

## 3 · Storm prep *(hurricane season)*
- **Hurricane shutters / panels** (local + national affiliates; OBX-specific installers exist, e.g. HurricaneShuttersOBX).
- **Portable & standby generators** (high AOV).
- **Smart water shutoff** (overlaps cat. 1 — pull a guest appearance here).
- **Surge protectors / whole-home surge** (post-outage breaker/appliance damage is a real OBX issue).
Post angle: *"Storm-prepping an OBX home you don't live in."*

## 4 · Smart-home (remote eyes & access)
- **Smart thermostats** (ecobee, Google Nest) — remote temp + humidity control; ties directly to the freeze/mold angles.
- **Smart locks** (August, Schlage Encode) — give cleaners/contractors/our crew time-boxed access.
- **WiFi cameras / video doorbell** (Ring, Wyze) — "looks lived-in," deters intrusion.
- **Water sensors** (overlap cat. 1).
Post angle: *"The 5 devices that let you watch your OBX house from 300 miles away."*

## Affiliate economics & approach
- **Amazon Associates**: home-improvement physical goods pay ~**3%** (most physical categories sit **1–4.5%**). Smart-home falls under tools/hardware. Low rate, but **high AOV** items (Flo $499, Phyn $850, dehumidifiers $200+, generators $400+) make 3% meaningful, and Amazon's cookie + cart-wide credit help.
- **Go beyond Amazon for the big-ticket items**: retailer programs (Home Depot, Lowe's via Impact) and **brand/direct programs** (SimpliSafe, Ring, Moen) often pay more per sale — worth applying once the Hub has traffic.
- **Compliance**: every page needs a clear **FTC affiliate disclosure**; Amazon requires specific disclosure language and prohibits quoting live prices (use "check current price").

## For Dev
Build reusable **product-card** and **comparison-table** components (name, image, one-line "why," CTA button) so Marketing can drop products into any post. Each buying guide ends with the same **house-watch CTA** block. Tag outbound links for affiliate IDs centrally so the network can be swapped later.
