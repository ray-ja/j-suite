# SOP 02 — Soft / Pressure Washing  *(SPINE · recurring routes · crew-run)*
**Who:** 2-person on any 2-story or roof work (ladder safety). **Why it matters:** the coast regrows algae every year — every wash is a recurring-plan candidate. Sell the route.

**Guardrail pricing:** house soft-wash 1-story ≤1,500 sqft $299 · ≤2,500 $399 · 2-story 2,500–3,500 $549 · roof soft-wash from $399 · driveway $99/$149/$199 by size. **Recurring 20% off.** House wash 3–5 hrs; driveway 1–2 hrs.

## Know the difference (lead with the right one)
- **Soft wash** = low pressure (~100 PSI) + SH/surfactant mix. **Roofs, siding, stucco, screens, painted surfaces.** Kills algae/mildew, lasts longer.
- **Pressure wash** = high pressure. **Concrete only** — driveways, sidewalks, dumpster pads, decks (carefully). **Never pressure-wash shingles** (strips the granules) — roofs are soft-wash, always.

## Before you start
- ☐ Walk the property; note surfaces + size (confirm the quote tier)
- ☐ **Plant protection:** pre-wet + tarp/cover landscaping near the work; have the plant-rinse ready
- ☐ Water source (customer spigot) + buffer tank if flow is weak
- ☐ Mix the right ratio: siding ~2.5% SH; concrete/masonry steps up to ~4%; add surfactant ~1 oz/gal
- ☐ 2-story/roof: ladder footed on solid ground, stabilizer on, **second crew member spotting the ladder** — never solo on a ladder

## On-site sequence
- ☐ Pre-rinse surface + surrounding plants
- ☐ Apply solution low-pressure, work bottom-up, let it **dwell** (don't let it dry on the surface)
- ☐ Agitate/spot-treat heavy stains
- ☐ Rinse top-down; re-rinse all plants/landscaping thoroughly
- ☐ Basic cleanup — coil hoses, no chemical containers left out

## Included vs. not
**Included:** pre-rinse + plant protection, solution + dwell, rinse, spot-treat, basic cleanup.
**Quote separately (don't give away):** sealing/staining, gutter brightening, screen removal, heavy oil-stain treatment, deck refinishing.

## Guards
- **PPE on for SH:** chem gloves, eye protection, and on roofs face shield + non-slip footwear. Mind overspray onto cars/neighbors.
- 3-story, steep, or anything past safe ladder reach → **refer**, don't improvise.
- Recurring close: every one-off wash → offer the annual plan before you leave; log it for Ray.
