# SOP 04 — Junk Removal / Clear-Outs  *(SPINE · crew-run · 2-person)*
**Who:** 2-person standard. **Why it matters:** most junk jobs are clean, fast, high-margin — and the leftovers (good furniture, tools, extension cords) are **free resale** = a real secondary revenue stream. Keep junk a priority. *This SOP carries the hard-won lessons — follow them and the job runs itself.*

**Guardrail pricing:** $350–850 typical · whole-house clear-out $1,200–2,500 · price on site by **volume (truck/trailer-bed fraction)** + dump fees + access. Flat per job, never hourly.

## Intake / before you roll
- ☐ **Bed-bug question (scripted, not rude):** *"Standard question for anything with fabric — any history of bed bugs?"* Carry zip mattress/sofa bags + sealed contractor bags + gloves. **Right to decline or surcharge infested items** — one infestation can contaminate the truck and every job after it. Non-negotiable.
- ☐ **Furniture sizing — don't undersize.** A "love seat" is a 2–3 seater; sofas/sectionals/recliners eat space. Confirm counts and real sizes so the load fits (the "love seat = small chair" miss cost us truck space).
- ☐ **Access assessment:** stairs, floors, long carry, driveway blocked by cars → **access-difficulty bump** in the price.
- ☐ **Travel/zone:** remote north → higher minimum + travel line; bundle remote stops.
- ☐ **One scheduled trip.** Set up front: this clearing is one trip; extra trips are billed. (Repeat callbacks are the margin killer.)

## Move-outs — hard sequencing policy
- ☐ **Movers finish FIRST → then junk → then cleaners.** Never work around active movers. **Confirm the movers are DONE before we roll** — no repeated callbacks while they're still going.
- ☐ **"Authorized for disposal" sign-off:** get explicit confirmation (text/app) that everything remaining is cleared to toss. Removes keep-vs-toss guessing and protects us from "you threw away my X." If there's no clear call on an item, **set it aside, don't toss it.**

## On-site work
- ☐ Walk the full scope with the customer; confirm what's going
- ☐ Protect floors/walls on the carry-out (blankets, straps)
- ☐ Load smart; **set aside resaleable items** (log them — resale is real money, but never take anything not authorized)
- ☐ Separate clean vegetative/recyclable from C&D where it saves dump fees
- ☐ Sweep/broom the cleared space — leave it better than found
- ☐ Before/after photos for the review ask

## Disposal
- Price the **dump fee into the job** every time: estimate the load, add tonnage (~$73.16/ton mixed C&D, first 500 lb free; clean veg = free), then labor + margin on top. This is the line new haulers eat by accident.

## Close out
- ☐ Confirm the customer's happy with the cleared space (photo walk-through)
- ☐ Invoice through the app + drop the review link
- ☐ Note any resale items recovered

## Guards
- Hazardous (paint, chemicals, propane, fuel) → **don't haul**; flag for separate handling.
- Over the two-person ceiling (huge hoarder load, needs a third body / multiple days) → price to **sub** or stage over scheduled billed trips — flag to Ray, don't absorb it.
- Disorganized customer pushing extra trips/scope → hold the line politely; one trip quoted, more is billed.
