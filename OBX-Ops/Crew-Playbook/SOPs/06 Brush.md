# SOP 06 — Small / Medium Ground Brush  *(SECONDARY · crew one-off)*
**Who:** 2-person. **Why it matters:** brush + overgrowth clearing leans on the owned STIHL AP platform and ties into lot cleanup and yard routes. **Strictly ground-based, within the reach cap.**

**Guardrail pricing:** quote by area / crew-day (~$600–900/crew-day basis) + any rental + disposal. Flat per job.

## Hard scope cap (read first)
- **IN:** ground-based brush, overgrowth, saplings, limbs/branches and **small trees reachable from the ground or the 30-ft ladder.**
- **OUT → refer:** climbing, large trees / big trunks, anything above ~30 ft or needing a controlled drop. *We are not a tree-removal service.*

## Before you start
- ☐ Walk the area; identify property lines, utilities, septic, irrigation, anything to protect
- ☐ Spot hazards: power lines (stay well clear), ground hornets/snakes, poison ivy, soft/wet ground
- ☐ Confirm what stays vs. goes with the customer (mark keepers)
- ☐ Gear: AP chainsaw/pole saw, loppers, machete, rakes, tarps; rent brush mower/chipper if the volume calls for it (pass-through line)

## On-site work
- ☐ PPE: **chainsaw chaps whenever the saw runs**, eye + ear protection, gloves, boots; hard hat if overhead limbs
- ☐ Cut low-to-high, drag to a staging pile, feed chipper or load for haul
- ☐ Keep **vegetative debris clean/separate → dumps FREE** (don't mix with trash/C&D)
- ☐ Rake and clear the worked area; haul off
- ☐ Before/after photos

## Before you commit a price
- ☐ Density + area (how thick, how big)
- ☐ Rental needed? (brush mower / chipper / stump grinder — stump grinder still rented ~$85–160/day)
- ☐ Disposal volume (free if clean veg; chipping cuts haul trips)
- ☐ Access + travel/zone bump

## Guards
- Anything past the reach cap, near power lines, or needing a climber → **stop and refer.** No exceptions.
- Bigger than a crew-day or needs a third body → price to sub / flag Ray.
