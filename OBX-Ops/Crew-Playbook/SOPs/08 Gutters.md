# SOP 08 — Gutter Cleaning  *(SECONDARY · crew one-off · ladder-safety SOP)*
**Who:** **2-person always** (ladder work — one works, one foots/spots the ladder). **Why it matters:** easy attach to washing, home-watch, and yard routes; absentee homes clog every season.

**Guardrail pricing:** by linear footage / stories + access. Flat per job. Bundle with a wash for an easy upsell.

## Hard scope cap (read first)
- **IN:** 1- and **2-story** gutters reachable from the 30-ft ladder or a water-fed pole.
- **OUT → refer:** 3-story, steep/unsafe roof pitch, or anything past safe ladder reach. **No working off the roof edge on a sketchy pitch.**

## Ladder safety (non-negotiable — this is why it's a 2-person job)
- ☐ Ladder on **firm, level ground**; stabilizer/standoff on so it rests off the gutter, not crushing it
- ☐ **Second crew member foots and holds the ladder** the entire time someone is on it
- ☐ 3-point contact; never overreach — get down and move the ladder instead
- ☐ Stay clear of power lines / service drops at the roofline
- ☐ Wet/windy → reschedule; don't push it

## On-site work
- ☐ Lay tarps under work zones to catch debris (protect plantings/hardscape)
- ☐ Scoop debris by hand/trowel into a bucket; bag it
- ☐ **Flush each run** with the hose toward the downspout; confirm water flows through
- ☐ Clear/confirm downspouts (snake or flush blockages); check splash blocks/extensions
- ☐ Quick look for gutter damage, loose hangers, leaks → note for Ray (don't repair, that's quote-separate)
- ☐ Rake up fallen debris; haul (clean veg = free disposal)
- ☐ Before/after photos

## Before you commit a price
- ☐ Linear feet + stories + roof pitch (safety = the cap)
- ☐ Access + travel/zone bump
- ☐ Offer the recurring/seasonal plan + bundle with washing

## Guards
- Past ladder reach / 3-story / unsafe pitch → **refer.**
- Gutter repair, guards install, fascia work = separate quote / possible refer (not a clean-out).
