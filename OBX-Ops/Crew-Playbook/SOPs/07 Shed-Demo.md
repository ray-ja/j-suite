# SOP 07 — Small Shed Demo  *(SECONDARY · crew one-off · capped)*
**Who:** 2-person. **Why it matters:** demo is in the crew's wheelhouse (clearing/grunt work) and pairs with junk haul-off. **Tightly capped — small structures only.**

**Guardrail pricing:** quote by structure size + haul/disposal + access. Flat per job.

## Hard scope cap (read first)
- **IN:** small single-story sheds/outbuildings (**≈ ≤10×12**), wood/metal, hand-demo + haul.
- **OUT → refer/sub:** anything with **live electrical or plumbing** still connected, structures on a **slab needing breaking**, anything requiring **permits or asbestos/lead handling** (older sheds/siding can contain it), multi-story, or attached to the house. If in doubt about age/materials → **stop and flag Ray.**

## Before you start
- ☐ Confirm utilities **disconnected** (power/water) — do NOT demo a shed with live service; flag to Ray to arrange disconnect
- ☐ Check age/materials — pre-1990 or suspect siding/roofing → asbestos/lead risk → **stop, refer for testing**
- ☐ Confirm the slab/foundation stays (we don't break concrete) or it's a refer
- ☐ Locate property lines, septic, irrigation, buried utility (call 811 if any digging) 
- ☐ Permit check: small accessory-structure demo is usually fine, but if the customer/locale needs a permit, that's a Ray/owner call before we start

## On-site work
- ☐ PPE: gloves, eye protection, hard hat, sturdy boots, dust mask (demo dust)
- ☐ De-construct top-down: roof → walls → frame → floor; pull nails/screws, watch for hidden hazards
- ☐ Sort: clean wood vs. mixed C&D vs. metal (metal may be resale/scrap)
- ☐ Load + haul; **C&D debris pays tonnage** (~$73.16/ton) — price it in (this is NOT free veg)
- ☐ Rake the pad/area clean; before/after photos

## Guards
- Live utilities, slab-breaking, asbestos/lead suspicion, permits, or anything bigger than the cap → **refer or escalate to Ray** before touching it.
- Over a crew-day or needs heavy equipment → price to sub.
