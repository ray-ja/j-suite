# SOP 01 — Home-Watch  *(SPINE · recurring · crew-run)*
**Who:** one crew member is fine. **Why it matters:** cheapest customer we can land — once we're the trusted eyes on a property, the washing, junk, gutters, and yard work follow. Sell the recurring plan.

**Guardrail pricing:** monthly $50/visit · bi-weekly $45 · weekly $40 · one-off $75 · storm check $95. Recurring = the goal. ~30–45 min/visit incl. drive. Near-zero materials.

## Before you roll
- ☐ Confirm address + **gate/alarm codes + key/lockbox** (log on the customer record, not loose paper)
- ☐ Know the plan (what this owner pays for) + any add-ons (package pickup, contractor meet-in)
- ☐ Phone charged — **every visit ends in a photo/text report**

## On-site checklist — walk it the same way every time
**Exterior**
- ☐ Walk the full perimeter — roof/eaves, siding, foundation, windows, doors
- ☐ Storm/wind damage, fallen limbs, debris
- ☐ Signs of pests/animals, wasp nests, standing water
- ☐ Doors & windows secure; no break-in signs
- ☐ Decks/rails, exterior lights, HVAC condenser unit

**Interior**
- ☐ Each room: leaks/water stains (ceilings, under sinks, water heater), smell for mold/musty
- ☐ Run water / flush toilets; check no slow drains
- ☐ HVAC running, thermostat at the set point (humidity control matters off-season)
- ☐ Breaker panel normal, no tripped breakers; fridge/freezer if owner asked
- ☐ Pests (droppings, ants, roaches), any alarms/leak detectors

**Add-ons (only if on the plan):** packages/mail, contractor meet-in, pre/post-storm prep, utilities on/off for guests.

## Close out
- ☐ Re-lock, reset alarm, return key to lockbox
- ☐ **Send the photo + text report** (2–6 photos + a one-line "all good" or the issue found)
- ☐ Found a problem? Note it, photograph it, and flag to Ray — **do not authorize repairs** (owner decision)
- ☐ Mark visit complete in the app

## Guards
- Anything that looks like real damage or a safety issue → document + escalate to Ray same day; don't try to fix it.
- Keep codes/keys controlled — never leave them in the open or text them in plain view.
