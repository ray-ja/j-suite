# SOP 03 — Mowing / Yard Routes  *(SPINE · recurring · crew-run)*
**Who:** crew (1–2 depending on lot). **Why it matters:** recurring routes = predictable weekly cash, and they pair with home-watch + washing so one crew keeps the whole property. Built on the owned **STIHL AP battery platform** (mower/trimmer/edger/blower/hedge/pole-saw add-ons).

**Guardrail pricing:** mow + trim + edge from $45/visit (recurring 20% off) · hedge trimming from $89 · debris cleanup & haul from $99 · large/oversized lot from $69/visit. Flat per visit, never hourly.

## Standard visit
- ☐ Mow at the right height (don't scalp coastal lawns)
- ☐ String-trim fence lines, beds, obstacles
- ☐ Edge hard surfaces (walks, driveway)
- ☐ Blow clippings off all hardscape — leave it cleaner than found
- ☐ Quick walk for trash/limbs; bag and haul what's there
- ☐ Photo the finished yard for the route log

## Add-on work (within scope)
- **Hedge/shrub trimming**, **leaf/debris cleanup**, **mulch refresh** (mulch billed as a materials line, cost + markup).
- **Limb/branch + small-tree work:** in scope **only if reachable from the ground or the 30-ft ladder — no climbing, no large trees.** Use the AP chainsaw/pole saw. Anything bigger or up high → refer to a tree service (note it for Ray).

## Disposal — the margin edge
- **Clean vegetative debris (brush, leaves, grass, pine straw) dumps FREE** at the Dare County C&D landfill / Public Works recycling. **Keep green waste clean and separate** — the second you mix in bagged trash or lumber you lose the free rate and pay $73.16/ton.

## Before you commit a price
- ☐ Lot size + slope + obstacle density (sizing the visit)
- ☐ **Travel/zone:** remote north (Corolla/Carova) → higher minimum + travel line; bundle remote stops on one route day
- ☐ Recurring vs one-off — push the recurring plan

## Guards
- Ear + eye protection on all powered equipment; label gas cans (right mix — wrong fuel kills 2-strokes; AP tools are battery, but check any gas backups).
- Over a crew-day or needs a third body (big overgrown lot) → price to sub / flag Ray, don't absorb it.
