# SOP 05 — Cleanup (Residential + Light-Commercial)  *(SECONDARY · crew one-off, recurring-able)*
**Who:** crew (1–2). **Why it matters:** litter/debris/dumpster-area/storefront cleanup is an easy recurring add for PMs, HOAs, and businesses — documented and scheduled, it "just gets handled."

**Guardrail pricing:** priced by lot size / scope · recurring plans 20% off (daily/weekly/monthly). Flat per visit.

## Scope
- Parking-lot & roadside litter, dumpster-area cleanup, storefront/sidewalk debris, post-event cleanup, light grounds policing.
- **Recurring is the play** — sell the scheduled plan and document each visit with photos.

## On-site checklist
- ☐ Walk the area; note hazards (broken glass, sharps, biohazard → gloves/grabber, don't hand-pick)
- ☐ High-vis vest + cones if near traffic/lot drive lanes
- ☐ Litter/debris pick + bag (contractor bags)
- ☐ Dumpster area: pick stray debris, sweep pad, push-broom corners
- ☐ Storefront: sidewalk sweep, entry debris, gum/stain spot-treat if quoted
- ☐ Haul bagged waste; confirm where it goes (customer dumpster vs. our haul + dump fee)
- ☐ Before/after photos → send with the visit log (this is what makes recurring sticky)

## Before you commit a price
- ☐ Lot/area size + how trashed it is (frequency drives the recurring price)
- ☐ Disposal: customer's dumpster (free) vs. we haul (add dump fee)
- ☐ Travel/zone bump for remote sites; bundle into a route

## Guards
- Biohazard/sharps/hazmat beyond basic litter → don't handle; flag to Ray.
- One-off that balloons into a haul-off → re-quote as a junk job (SOP 04), don't eat it.
