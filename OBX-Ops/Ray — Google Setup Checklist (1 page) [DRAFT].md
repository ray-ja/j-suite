# Ray — Google Setup Checklist
*Three owner-actions to make reviews live. ~30 min total. Do these in order. Draft.*

These are the steps only **you** can do (they need your Google login + website access). Once done, every review template, the QR card, and the standalone QR all work.

### ☐ 1. Claim & optimize the Google Business Profile
Follow Marketing's step-by-step in **`Marketing/Launch-Day Playbook.md`** (GBP section). At minimum: claim/verify "OBX Lot Solutions," set service area (Corolla→Manteo), categories (Pressure Washing, Junk Removal, Property Maintenance), hours, photos, phone **(252) 564-8717**, and website.
→ *Then grab your direct review link* (GBP → "Ask for reviews"), format `https://g.page/r/XXXX/review`. **Copy it.**

### ☐ 2. Set the `/review` redirect
On the website, create **`obxlotsolutions.com/review`** and point it at the Google review link from Step 1.
*Why: it's clean to print/say, and if the Google link ever changes you only fix it here — the QR and printed cards never have to change.*

### ☐ 3. Wire it to the QR card + templates
- The **Review Card** and **Review QR** in `OBX-Ops/` already point to `obxlotsolutions.com/review` — once Step 2 is live, they work as-is. Print the card; start leaving it on jobs.
- Paste the review link into the **text + email templates** (`Review Request Kit`) so `[REVIEW LINK]` is filled in.
- Add the QR to your invoice and email signature.
