# Cold-Call Script — House-Watch & Washing  *(DRAFT)*
For the **Top 15 PM Targets** list. Pairs with the existing Cold Call Playbook (lot/dumpster version) — this one leads with **washing + house-watch** for property/rental managers.

**Goal of every call:** not to sell — book a **free 5-minute walkthrough** so you can quote. Easy yes.
**Your edge, say it every time:** *reliable, professional, shows up.* Rare on OBX; everyone knows it. That's why people switch.
**Best time:** Tue–Thu, 9–11am or 1:30–4pm. Avoid Mon AM / Fri PM.

---

## A · Opener — get to the right person
> "Hi, this is **Ray with OBX Lot Solutions** — we do exterior soft-washing and property checks for vacation-rental and absentee-owned homes here on the Outer Banks. Are you the right person to talk to about keeping your managed homes clean and looked-after?"

If **yes** → pitch. If **no** → *"No problem — who handles that, and what's the best way to reach them?"* (get name + ext, log **GK**.)

## B · Pitch — pick the lead by who you're calling *(15 sec, then stop talking)*

**▶ Big rental managers (Resort, Southern Shores, Stan White, KEES, Sun, OBX Blue, Village, Beach):** lead with **washing.**
> "Salt and humidity grow algae on every coastal home every year — and a green, streaky house is the complaint owners and guests notice first. We soft-wash homes on a **recurring schedule** so your portfolio always looks rental-ready and you never field that call. We're local, uniformed, and we actually show up. Recurring accounts get **20% off**. Could I look at one or two of your homes this week and put together a no-pressure quote?"

**▶ HOA / residential PMs & smaller shops (Joe Lamb, Seaside, Signature Touch, Harrell, Cove):** lead with **house-watch.**
> "Most of the homes out here sit empty for stretches, and a lot of insurers now **require** check-ins on vacant ones. We do scheduled property checks — walk the home inside and out, run water, check HVAC, look for leaks, pests, and storm damage, and send a **photo report** every visit. Add soft-washing when it needs it. Reliable, uniformed, recurring. Could I do a quick walkthrough of one property this week so I can quote it?"

Then **be quiet** and let them answer.

## C · The ask
- "What day works — Wednesday or Thursday better?"
- Lock a time. Get the **property address** and their **cell/email** for the quote.
- "Great — I'll be there [day/time] in uniform, and you'll have a written quote the same day."

## D · Anchor numbers *(only if pushed — don't lead with price)*
- **House-watch:** monthly **$50/visit** · bi-weekly **$45** · weekly **$40** · one-off **$75** · storm check **$95**. (~30–45 min/visit, photo report each time.)
- **Soft-wash house:** ≤1,500 sqft **$299** · ≤2,500 **$399** · 2-story 2,500–3,500 **$549** · roof from **$399** · driveway **$99/$149/$199** by size.
- **Recurring = 20% off.** Always offer it — recurring is the whole point.

## F · Objection handling
**"We already have someone."**
> "Totally fair — most managers do, until that someone no-shows in peak season. Let me leave you a quote so you've got a reliable backup on file. Costs nothing to have it."

**"Our owners/HOA handle that themselves."**
> "Makes sense for some. The ones who travel or live out of state are the ones who want a local set of eyes — that's exactly who we're built for. Want me to quote it so you can offer it to them?"

**"How much?"**
> "Depends on the home's size and how often — that's why I'd rather look first so I'm not guessing. Five minutes on-site and you'll have an exact number, no obligation. Recurring gets 20% off."

**"No budget right now."**
> "Understood — recurring is built for that, and one missed leak or a green house costs a lot more than a check-in. Let me quote it so you can decide with a real number."

**"Send me an email."**
> "Happy to drop a one-pager by in person so you can put a face to the name — I'm in [their town] this week anyway. What's a good time?"

**"Call me back later."**
> "No problem — later this week or early next?" *(Get a specific day. Log it. Actually call back.)*
