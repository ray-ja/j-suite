# Hauling Equipment — Rent vs Buy (Options & Pricing)

*Rebuilt with current 2026 pricing and saved to the project folder (the earlier version stayed in another session's outputs and didn't carry over). This pairs with the Job Cost Model and the Monday Quote Kit — it's the "how do we actually move the junk" layer. There's a **Home Depot in Kill Devil Hills**, so Load 'N Go is local to you.*

**Bottom line:** you have three ways to haul, and the right one depends on load size. Rent for small/occasional, dumpster for whole-house, buy a trailer once volume is steady.

---

## The three options, and when each wins

| Method | Best for | Your cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Roll-off dumpster** | Whole-house / estate clear-outs (the June 17 job) | ~$300–500/wk, **disposal included** | No towing, no dump runs. Customer-sited. The move for big jobs. |
| **Rent truck/trailer** (Home Depot / U-Haul) | Small–medium loads, one-offs, before you own a trailer | ~$20–60/day + dump fee | You still pay the C&D landfill ($73.16/ton). Cheap to start, zero ownership cost. |
| **Buy your own trailer** | Once junk volume is steady (your flagged gating item) | one-time $1k–9k | Stops the per-job rental bleed; a dump trailer saves your back. Breakeven math below. |

---

## Home Depot — Load 'N Go (Kill Devil Hills)

- **Pickup (8' bed):** ~**$17.99** first 75 min
- **Cargo van / F-250 flatbed:** ~**$19** first 75 min; single-day & weekly rates also offered (unlimited-miles tier advertised from ~$29)
- **Utility trailers (3×5, 5×8):** ~**$20–44/day** or **$80–170/week**
- **Watch-outs:** overtime kicks in once the 75-min window closes (these are built for quick runs, not all-day), **$150 refundable deposit**, must **refuel** on return, need **21+, license, proof of liability insurance**.
- **Use it for:** a single quick dump run or hauling a mid-size load when a dumpster is overkill.

## U-Haul

- **Pickup / cargo van / 10' box truck:** **$19.95/day** base **+ mileage** ($0.99–1.39/mi on trucks)
- **Utility & cargo trailers:** **$14.95–34.95/day**, **unlimited miles** (trailers don't get the per-mile charge — better deal than the trucks for towing)
- Realistic all-in for a local day with mileage/gas/fees ≈ **$150** for a truck; a trailer is far cheaper if you tow with your own truck.
- **Use it for:** towing a trailer-load with your own truck at low cost, or a box truck when you need real volume without a dumpster.

## Buy your own trailer (the gating item)

- **Basic utility trailer (5×8–6×12):** roughly **$1,000–2,500** new — confirm locally.
- **Hydraulic dump trailer:** roughly **$5,000–9,000** — the real upgrade for junk (tip the load, don't shovel it).
- **Breakeven vs renting:** at ~$40/day rental, a $1,500 utility trailer pays for itself in ~**38 rental-days**. If junk becomes 2–3 jobs/week, that's a single season. A dump trailer is justified once you're running steady clear-outs and want to spare the crew the unload.

---

## How to use this in a quote
- **Whole house →** price a **roll-off dumpster** (Soundside Recycling, Dare) as a pass-through line. (Monday job = this.)
- **Smaller load →** rent a Home Depot/U-Haul **trailer** + your truck, add the **$73.16/ton** dump fee, then labor + margin.
- Until you own a trailer, **build the rental cost into every junk quote** so it never eats your margin.
