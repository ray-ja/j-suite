## SEO conventions (apply to every post)
- Each file has meta title, description, primary + secondary keywords, slug, tags, and featured-image alt at the top — lift directly.
- **One H1 per post**; use the H2s as written (they carry secondary keywords).
- **Internal links** are marked in `[brackets]` — wire each to its Hub page (Storm Prep, Insurance & Vacancy, Off-Season Care, Smart Monitoring, Should-You-Hire-Home-Watch) and to the OBX Lot Solutions service pages where noted.
- **Every post ends in the checklist lead-capture** (the email magnet → House-Watch sequence) as the primary CTA, with a soft service link as secondary.
- **Cross-link posts to each other** (they reference siblings) — builds topical authority and dwell time.
