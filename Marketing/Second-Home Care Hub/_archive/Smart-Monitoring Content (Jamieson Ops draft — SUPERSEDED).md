## Page 1 — Pillar page: "Smart Monitoring for Your Empty Outer Banks Home"

**H1:** Know your second home is safe — even when you're 800 miles away.

**Opening (the fear, named plainly):**
A second home on the Outer Banks sits empty most of the year. The most expensive problems — water leaks, HVAC failure, storm damage, intrusion — don't announce themselves. By the time a neighbor or a monthly check-in notices, a $40 part has become a $40,000 insurance claim. Smart monitoring closes that gap: real-time alerts the moment something's wrong, plus a local pro who can actually go fix it.

**The four things every empty home should watch (section blocks):**

1. **Water — the #1 silent destroyer.**
   Water damage is the most common and most expensive thing that happens to an unoccupied home. A supply line behind a fridge or a failed water heater can run for weeks. We install leak sensors at every wet point and an automatic shutoff valve that kills the water the instant a leak is detected — and texts you. *Bonus: many insurers discount premiums for monitored water shutoff.*

2. **Temperature & humidity — the climate killer.**
   An HVAC failure you don't know about means frozen pipes in a cold snap or mold blooming in OBX summer humidity. Temperature and humidity sensors alert you the moment the house drifts out of safe range, so you catch a dead AC before it becomes a mold remediation bill.

3. **Doors & access — keyless, trackable.**
   Smart locks mean no hidden keys, no lockbox guesswork. Issue a code to a cleaner, a contractor, or yourself — and see exactly who came and went, and when. Codes expire on their own.

4. **Eyes on the property — exterior cameras.**
   Exterior cameras at entries and the driveway (never inside — privacy first) let you and us see storm damage, check on a contractor, or confirm a delivery, from anywhere.

**The piece everyone forgets — the internet has to stay up:**
Every one of those sensors is useless if the internet drops — and OBX power and internet go down exactly when you need monitoring most: during a storm. We back the whole system with **Starlink**, so your home keeps reporting even when the grid and the cable company don't. *This is the detail box-store DIY kits miss, and it's why we exist.*

**The close (funnel to package):**
We don't sell you a box of gadgets and wish you luck. We design it, install it, and **monitor it for you** — one local Outer Banks company, one number to call, one monthly plan that keeps it all running. → **[See the Rental-Ready Tech package]** / **[Get a free monitoring assessment]**

## Page 3 — "Why Remote Monitoring Needs Backup Internet (the Starlink angle)"

**H1:** Monitoring that goes dark in a storm isn't monitoring.

- The problem: every smart-home and home-watch monitoring setup depends on the home's internet. On the OBX, the power and the cable go out precisely during the events you most need to watch for.
- The fix: Starlink as primary or automatic-failover backup, hardwired into the network, so alerts keep flowing during outages.
- This is Jamieson's genuine differentiator and ties the Second-Home Hub directly to the rental-tech package. Owners who get this are pre-sold on the upgrade.
- CTA: **[Storm-proof your monitoring]**
