# OBX Second-Home Care Hub — Graphics Concept

*Light visual direction for Dev/design. Defines the "OBX-trust" palette, the hero concept, the section icon system, type, and imagery direction. Concept mockups (`graphics/hero-concept.svg`, `graphics/section-icons.svg`) accompany this doc. **Draft — direction, not final art.***

---

## Design intent

The Hub should feel **trustworthy, calm, and unmistakably coastal** — closer to a respected regional resource than a hard-sell affiliate blog. The reader is a worried owner far from home; the design's job is to lower their pulse and signal "you can rely on this." Clean, airy, lots of white space, confident type, restrained color. Editorial, not salesy.

It's a cousin of the OBX Lot Solutions brand (shared navy + green connective accent) but its own thing — more editorial, with a coastal palette that reads "resource," not "service company."

---

## The "OBX-trust" palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trust / authority | Deep navy | `#14233F` | Headlines, nav, footer, primary text on light |
| Calm / water | Coastal teal | `#2E8BA6` | Links, accents, icon strokes, secondary buttons |
| Approachable / local | Warm sand | `#E8DCC4` | Section backgrounds, cards, warmth |
| Connective accent (OBX) | OBX green | `#8BC34A` | Primary CTA buttons, key highlights — used sparingly |
| Background | Off-white | `#F7FAFC` | Page background |
| Body ink | Slate | `#243044` | Body copy |
| Muted | Cool grey | `#5B6678` | Captions, meta, microcopy |

**Color logic:** navy + sand carry the page (trust + warmth); teal is the calm coastal thread through links and icons; OBX green is reserved almost entirely for the *primary action* (checklist CTA) so the eye always finds the next step. Green is the visual handshake back to OBX Lot Solutions.

**Accessibility:** maintain AA contrast — navy/ink on off-white and sand; white text only on navy/teal/green fills (not on sand).

---

## Typography

- **Headings:** a confident humanist serif *or* a strong geometric sans (e.g., a Fraunces/Source Serif feel for editorial trust, or Inter/Work Sans for clean modern). Recommend **serif headings + sans body** for that "trusted guide" editorial feel.
- **Body:** clean sans, generous line-height (1.6), comfortable measure (~65–75 chars). Readability is a trust signal for a worried reader.
- **Hierarchy:** big calm H1, clear H2 section breaks, pull-quotes for the reassuring lines.

## Hero concept (see `graphics/hero-concept.svg`)

- **Layout:** left-aligned headline + subhead + two CTAs over a calm coastal scene; airy, uncluttered.
- **Imagery:** a serene OBX coastal home at soft light (dune grass, soft sky, water hint) — *calm and cared-for*, never a storm or damage. We sell peace of mind, so the hero shows the *good outcome*: a safe, beautiful home. (Real photography preferred at build; the SVG is a stylized stand-in showing composition + color.)
- **Overlay:** subtle navy gradient on the image so white headline text stays legible.
- **Primary CTA:** OBX-green button — "Get the free Home-Watch Checklist." **Secondary:** outline/teal text link.
- **Tone:** horizon line, gentle wave motif as a recurring graphic device (used as section dividers too).

## Section icon system (see `graphics/section-icons.svg`)

Six simple, consistent line icons (teal stroke on sand/white circle), one per content topic — friendly, not technical:

1. **Storm Prep** — cloud + shield
2. **Insurance & Vacancy** — document + checkmark
3. **Off-Season Care** — house + leaf/droplet (humidity)
4. **Hurricane Checklist** — clipboard/checklist
5. **Hire a Home-Watch** — house + eye / magnifier
6. **Smart Monitoring** — phone + signal/wifi

**Icon rules:** consistent stroke weight, rounded caps, same circular container, teal on light. Simple enough to also work tiny in nav and as list bullets.

## Recurring graphic devices

- **Wave divider** — a subtle single-line wave between sections (ties the coastal theme together, breaks up long content pages).
- **Pick card** — clean bordered card for affiliate recommendations (icon/name/why/caveat/price button) per the affiliate doc.
- **Reassurance pull-quotes** — the calming one-liners set large in navy on sand.
- **Trust footer band** — navy band with the disclosure, restating the "run by a local company" honesty.

## Imagery direction

- **Yes:** calm coastal homes in good light, tidy interiors, dune/beach/water serenity, real local detail.
- **No:** storm-damage imagery, stock-y "smiling agent," fear visuals, generic suburban houses. We're coastal, calm, and real.
