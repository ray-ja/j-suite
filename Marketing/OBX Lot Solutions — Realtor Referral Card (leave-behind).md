## Opening (to the agent)
Every house you sell leaves something behind — a garage full of the last owner's stuff, a tired driveway in the listing photos, or a vacant home nobody's checking on between closing and move-in. That's our work. You hand off one number; we make you look good.

## The three things we handle for your listings

**1. House & estate clear-outs.**
Sold a house the new owner wants emptied? We clear it out — every room, garage, shed, attic — haul it off, and sweep it clean. Donatable items dropped at a charity (receipt for your seller). Written quote the same day, work photo-documented start to finish.

**2. Pressure & soft washing — before the photos, before the closing.**
Salt and algae age a coastal house fast. A soft-washed roof and siding and a clean driveway lift curb appeal where it counts — the listing photos and the final walkthrough. We use low-pressure soft wash on roofs and siding (never strips shingles) and pressure on concrete.

**3. Home-watch on vacant & staged listings.**
While a home sits empty, we check it on a schedule — leaks, pests, storm damage, HVAC — and send a photo report every visit. Peace of mind for your absentee sellers, and many insurers require checks on vacant homes anyway.

## Why agents make us their go-to
One point of contact. We show up on time, in uniform, and leave a written quote the same day — no chasing, no surprises. You look like the agent who knows the right people. (Most of our work is recurring, so we'd rather earn the relationship than win one job.)
