# Marketing Strategy Brief — OBX Lot Solutions + Jamieson Automation

*The Marketing & Content lane's operating plan. This sits on top of the existing [Market Research & Strategy] and [Marketing Channel Plan] — it doesn't repeat the market sizing or the channel/cost tiers. Its job is to answer the questions those docs don't: who exactly we're talking to, how each brand sounds, what we put in front of people, and the order we ship it. Effort split is roughly 70% OBX / 30% Jamieson, because OBX is the near-term cash engine and Jamieson is the margin we layer on once the relationships exist.*

*Nothing in here sends without Ray's approval. Drafts get built; campaigns and sends wait for the go-ahead.*

---

## The single strategic idea everything serves

One customer base, sold twice. Property managers and absentee second-home owners on the Outer Banks need a reliable local. **OBX Lot Solutions earns the trust cheaply and recurringly (washing, house-watch). Jamieson sells the high-margin tech into that same trusted door (Starlink, locks, AV, lighting).** So our marketing isn't two campaigns — it's one relationship engine with two price tiers hanging off it. Every asset should either *open* a property-manager relationship or *deepen* one.

The wedge is **house-watch**. It's the lowest-friction reason for an absentee owner to let us onto the property, and once we're the trusted person walking that house, every other service follows. A meaningful slice of our content budget points at that single offer.

## OBX Lot Solutions — the cash engine (≈70% of effort)

### Positioning

> **The reliable local the Outer Banks runs out of.** We clean, wash, watch, and clear out properties for owners who aren't here and managers who can't find dependable help — and we show up, every time, with proof.

"Reliable" is the entire wedge. In a market with a real labor shortage and absentee owners who've been burned by no-shows, dependability *is* the product. Marketing leads with proof of reliability (photo reports, before/afters, on-time) more than with price.

### Who we're talking to

**Primary — Property Managers & HOAs.** They control dozens to hundreds of doors. One yes = recurring revenue across many properties. They care about: not having to chase a vendor, clean documentation, weekend turnaround, one point of contact. This is a *relationship and outreach* audience, not an ad audience — content's role here is credibility (a clean website, real reviews, a leave-behind), not lead capture.

**Secondary — Absentee second-home owners (~60% of OBX homes).** They live elsewhere, worry about their property, and many have insurers requiring vacant-home checks. They care about: peace of mind, photo proof, "someone I can trust while I'm away." This is the house-watch audience and the best fit for paid local ads and community-group content.

**Tertiary — Year-round residents** for one-off washing/junk jobs. Useful for reviews, yard-sign clustering, and cash between recurring accounts; not the strategic focus.

### Voice & tone

Plain-spoken, local, dependable. Talks like a competent neighbor, not a corporation or a hype-y ad. Short sentences. Proof over adjectives — "here's the driveway before and after" beats "premium quality service." Confident about reliability, never boastful. No jargon. Occasional dry warmth is fine; gimmicks are not.

Words we use: reliable, local, show up, on time, photo report, recurring, peace of mind, salt and algae, your property.
Words we avoid: cheap, world-class, synergy, unbeatable, #1.

### Palette & assets

- Green `#8BC34A` (energy / action — CTAs, accents) and Navy `#1B2A4E` (trust / headers).
- Logo + mark in `Brand assets/OBX Lot Solutions/`.
- Every visual leans on **real before/after photos** over stock. The photo workflow (below) is the most valuable marketing asset this brand has.

### Core offers & hooks (what we actually put in front of people)

| Offer | Hook | Primary audience |
|---|---|---|
| **House-watch (the wedge)** | "While you're away, we're checking in — with photo proof every visit." | Absentee owners, PMs |
| **Soft/pressure washing + recurring plan** | "The coast grows algae every year. Wash it once, then never think about it again." | Owners, residents |
| **PM vendor relationship** | "One reliable local for washing, watch, junk, and cleanups — across all your doors." | Property managers |
| **Junk removal / clean-outs** | "Rental clean-out or a garage full of junk — gone today." | PMs, residents |
| **Holiday lighting (Q4)** | "Book your install before Thanksgiving — we hang it, store it, and rehang next year." | Affluent owners |

### Content pillars (everything we make ladders to one of these)

1. **Proof** — before/after photos, photo reports, on-time receipts. The backbone. (~40% of output.)
2. **Education / local** — "soft wash vs pressure wash," "why coastal homes need annual washing," "what insurers require for vacant homes." Builds search authority and trust.
3. **Offers** — recurring plan, seasonal (holiday lighting, storm prep), referral asks.
4. **Reviews & social proof** — turning every happy job into a Google review and a community-group mention.

### Lead magnets

- **"OBX Absentee Owner's Home Checklist"** — a free, branded one-pager of what should be checked on a vacant coastal home each month (and how often). Captures email, positions house-watch as the obvious answer. *Highest-priority magnet.*
- **"Free lot/property quote"** — the existing quote workflow as a CTA; instant value, low friction.
- **PM leave-behind** — already have the flyer PDF; pair it with a one-page "what we do across your portfolio" sheet.

## Jamieson Automation — the margin layer (≈30% of effort)

### Positioning

> **Rental-ready tech, installed right by someone who actually knows the systems.** Starlink, wifi, smart locks, cameras, and AV — sold as working outcomes, not boxes, to the same OBX owners and managers who already trust us.

The differentiator is genuine expertise (Q-Sys/AV, networking, Starlink) in a market thin on real pros. Marketing sells *the working result and the expert behind it*, never the cheapest hardware.

### Who we're talking to

**Primary — Vacation-rental managers & owners** who need reliable guest wifi, keyless entry, and connectivity. Sold per door, ideally through the PM relationships OBX already built. This is the cross-sell.

**Secondary — Restaurants & bars** for commercial AV/Q-Sys. Relationship-sold, walk-in, high-ticket. Content's role is a credible website + a couple of case studies, not ads.

**Tertiary — Affluent homeowners** for permanent outdoor lighting, cameras, smart home. Search-intent and referral driven.

### Voice & tone

Expert, precise, reassuring. Speaks to people who've been burned by DIY or a cut-rate installer. Explains the *why* (wifi at the door is why your lock works) without condescending. Calm authority. Where OBX is "dependable neighbor," Jamieson is "the pro who gets it right the first time."

### Palette & assets

- Navy `#002052` (primary / trust) and Blue `#0099E5` (tech / accent).
- Logo in `Brand assets/Jamieson Automation/`.
- Visuals: clean install shots, tidy cable work, the app screen — proof of craftsmanship.

### Core offers & hooks

| Offer | Hook | Primary audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Rental-ready tech package** (Starlink + mesh wifi + smart lock + thermostat) | "Everything a guest needs to check in and stay connected — one install, per door." | Rental PMs/owners |
| **Starlink pro install** | "Reliable internet where cable can't reach — mounted clean, sealed right." | Owners, remote properties |
| **Commercial AV / Q-Sys** | "Multi-zone audio and paging done by someone who actually runs the platform." | Restaurants, bars |
| **Permanent outdoor LED lighting** | "Holiday, accent, and security lighting in one app-controlled install — year-round." | Affluent homes |

### Content pillars

1. **Expertise / education** — "why your smart lock keeps dropping (it's the wifi)," "PoE vs wifi cameras." Establishes the pro. (~50% of output — this is how Jamieson earns trust.)
2. **Outcome proof** — clean install galleries, the rental-ready bundle in action.
3. **Cross-sell** — content aimed at existing OBX PM contacts: "now that we watch your properties, here's the tech that makes them rent better."

### Lead magnets

- **"Rental-Ready Tech Checklist for OBX Hosts"** — what a modern vacation rental needs (wifi, keyless, connectivity) and why. Captures hosts/PMs, sets up the package.
- **Free site/signal assessment** — low-commitment entry to a high-ticket install.

## The shared engine: property-manager relationships + the photo workflow

Two assets do more for both brands than any single campaign:

**1. The PM relationship list.** OBX outreach and Jamieson cross-sell run off *one shared list* of property managers. Marketing's job is to make every touch credible: a clean website per brand, real Google reviews, a tight leave-behind, and a simple "portfolio services" one-pager that shows a PM both menus at once. We don't run ads at PMs — we make Ray's outreach land.

**2. The before/after photo workflow.** This is the highest-leverage thing this lane owns. Every job is content. The workflow:

- **On site:** consistent before + after shots, same angle, same framing. (A one-page shot guide goes in `Marketing/Photos/`.)
- **Capture:** photos land in `Marketing/Photos/before-after/`, named by `date_service_location`.
- **Repurpose:** each set becomes — a Google Business Profile post, a community-group post, a website gallery entry, a review request, and an ad creative. One job, five assets.
- **Approval:** drafts are staged; Ray approves before anything posts.

This turns work Ray is already doing into a continuous, free content stream — exactly the "spend sweat before dollars" rule from the channel plan.

## Campaign roadmap (OBX-weighted)

Sequenced to match the channel plan's cost tiers — free/sweat-equity first, paid as cash flows.

**Now (0–30 days) — stand up the proof machine, free:**
- Build the **OBX Absentee Owner's Home Checklist** lead magnet (drives house-watch).
- Stand up the **before/after photo workflow** + shot guide so every job starts generating content.
- Draft **Google Business Profile** content + a review-request script for both brands.
- Draft the **PM portfolio one-pager** (both menus, one sheet) to back Ray's outreach.
- Draft a starter **2-week content calendar** (proof + education posts for community groups/GBP).

**30–60 days — first recurring content + first paid dollar:**
- Launch the **house-watch campaign** (lead magnet + community posts + GBP), the wedge offer.
- Stand up **email sequences**: new-lead nurture, recurring-plan upsell, post-job review ask.
- Draft creative for **Google Local Services Ads** (OBX washing/cleanup) for when budget turns on.
- Begin **Jamieson education content** (the "it's your wifi" angle) to seed expertise.

**60–90 days — layer margin + seasonal:**
- **Rental-ready tech package** campaign to existing PM contacts (the cross-sell).
- **Pre-sell holiday lighting** (Sept/Oct push) — OBX off-season cash, bridges to Jamieson permanent lighting.
- Turn early jobs into **case studies / before-after galleries** feeding both websites (handed to the Dev lane to publish).
