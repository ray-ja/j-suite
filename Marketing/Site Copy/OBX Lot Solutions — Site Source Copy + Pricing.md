## FAQ (seed set)

- **Do you soft wash or pressure wash my roof?** Always soft wash on roofs and siding — high pressure strips roof granules and damages siding. Pressure is for concrete and hard surfaces only.
- **How often does a coastal home need washing?** About once a year out here — salt and humidity bring algae back. Recurring plans save 20%.
- **What's home-watch?** Scheduled checks on a vacant property — we walk it inside and out, run basic checks, and send you a photo report every visit.
- **Are you insured?** Yes — general liability coverage; certificate available to property managers on request.
- **Do you take everything when you clear a house?** Almost — we can't haul hazardous items (paint, chemicals, propane, fuel), but we'll flag them and point you to the right disposal.
- **Can I pay online?** Yes. Flat-rate services book and pay right on the site; larger jobs get a free written quote first.
