## LONG description (full GBP "Description" field, ~max 750 words — use as much as fits)

OBX Lot Solutions keeps Outer Banks properties clean, watched, and cared-for — without the owner having to chase anyone down. We're your local crew, professionally run: a uniformed team that shows up on a schedule and sends a photo report after every visit, so the work gets done whether or not you're here to see it.

Most of what we do is recurring, because reliable upkeep beats one-off scrambles on the coast:

• **Home-Watch Plans** — scheduled checks on your vacant home or rental. We walk the inside and outside, look for leaks, pests, storm damage, and break-ins, run the water, confirm the HVAC, and send you a photo report every visit. Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.

• **Wash Plans** — soft washing for roofs and siding (the safe, low-pressure method the coast needs) and pressure washing for driveways and concrete, on a schedule so salt and algae never get a foothold.

• **Yard-Care Plans** — mowing, trimming, and edging on a recurring schedule, so your property always looks cared-for.

We also handle one-off jobs when you need them: junk removal and full house or estate clear-outs, window washing, storm debris, and commercial lot and storefront cleanup.

Put your property on a plan and save 20% on every visit — one crew that already knows the place, a locked-in price, photo proof every time, and nothing for you to remember. Or call us for a single job; either way you get a free, written quote the same day.

We're a local company serving the entire Outer Banks — Corolla, Duck, Southern Shores, Kitty Hawk, Kill Devil Hills, Nags Head, Manteo, Hatteras Island, and lower Currituck — and we're insured ($1M general liability).

Call or text (252) 564-8717, or visit obxlotsolutions.com to start a plan or get a quote.
