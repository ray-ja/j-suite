## How to use this

- **Personalize the first line of every message** — name the company, a property, or something specific. Cold outreach that's obviously templated gets ignored; one real detail changes everything.
- **Sequence:** 3 touches over ~10 days, then stop and move on (or drop to a quarterly check-in). Don't over-chase.
- **Channel:** email where you have it; the DM versions are for Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn where you only have a social handle.
- **Goal of every message is small:** a 15-minute call or a walk-through of one property — not a sale in the inbox.
- **Pair with the leave-behind:** attach or reference "Why rental-ready tech sells your rentals" (the one-pager); Jamieson Ops handles the actual proposal/quote after interest.
- **Exit on reply:** anyone who responds comes out of the sequence and gets handled personally.

## Targeting notes

- **Best-fit PMs:** vacation-rental managers with multiple doors on the OBX; independent/boutique managers are often more reachable than the big franchises. Owner-operators of 2–10 rentals also convert well.
- **The cross-sell angle:** where OBX Lot Solutions already has a relationship (washing/house-watch), Jamieson outreach is *warm* — reference it: "We already handle property care for some managers out here; the tech side is the same trusted hands." Run both off the one shared PM list.
- **What success looks like:** not an inbox sale — a walk-through booked. One property proven → the whole portfolio follows.
- **Handoff:** interest → Jamieson Ops builds the per-door proposal/quote; this kit's job is to open the door.
