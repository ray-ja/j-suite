# PART 1 — Google Business Profile (GBP) setup + optimization

**Why this is first:** roughly half of Google searches are local, and the "map pack" at the top of local results is driven almost entirely by Google Business Profiles. A complete, active profile with reviews is the difference between showing up for "pressure washing near me" and being invisible. It's free, and it compounds. **Ray creates and verifies it** (Google requires the business owner); this guide makes that fast and complete.

## A. Create & verify

1. Go to **google.com/business** and sign in with the business Google account (use a dedicated one, e.g. the contact@ inbox — not a personal account).
2. Enter the business name exactly: **OBX Lot Solutions** (no extra keywords — Google penalizes keyword-stuffed names).
3. Choose **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** → this makes it a **service-area business** (you go to them; you don't have a storefront). Hide the home address if that's where you're based.
4. Set the **service area** by listing the towns you cover (see list in section C).
5. **Verify** — Google will offer phone, email, or video verification. Video is increasingly required for service-area businesses: a short clip showing your truck/equipment, signage/branding, and that you operate in the area. Have the branded truck magnets/shirts ready for this.

## B. Categories (this drives what you rank for)

- **Primary category:** `Pressure washing service` — this is the cash engine, make it primary.
- **Additional categories (add all that fit):** `Cleaning service`, `Gutter cleaning service`, `Junk removal service`, `House cleaning service`, `Property maintenance`. Add `Window cleaning service` if offered.
- *Don't add categories for things you don't do — it dilutes ranking and invites mismatched leads.*

## C. Complete every field (Google rewards completeness)

- **Phone:** (252) 207-5985 · **Website:** obxlotsolutions.com · **Email** where prompted.
- **Service area:** list your real coverage — e.g. Kitty Hawk, Kill Devil Hills, Nags Head, Southern Shores, Duck, Corolla, Manteo, Wanchese, Colington, and any others you serve. (Confirm the exact list with Ray.)
- **Hours:** set real hours; add **special hours** for holidays. Even a service business should show hours.
- **Services:** add each one with a short description and price-from where comfortable — pull straight from the Service Guide (soft wash, pressure wash, house-watch, junk removal, gutter cleaning, etc.). This feeds search.
- **Business description (750 chars):** draft below — paste into the "from the business" field.
- **Attributes:** check relevant ones (e.g. "Online estimates," "Identifies as veteran-owned" if Ray wants that — it's a genuine trust signal given his background).
- **Opening date:** set it so the profile shows established history.

## D. Photos (this is where your Shot Guide pays off)

- Add a **logo** and a **cover photo** (a clean branded truck shot or a strong before/after).
- Upload **before/after sets** from the photo workflow — Google favors profiles with fresh, real photos, and they're your best proof. Aim to add new ones every week.
- Add **team/truck/equipment** photos for authenticity (and they double as video-verification material).

## E. Reviews — the #1 ranking and trust lever

- The day a job wraps, **ask for a Google review** and text the customer the direct review link (Google generates a short one in the dashboard). This is the highest-leverage 30 seconds in the whole business.
- **Respond to every review**, good or bad — thank the good ones by name, address the rare bad one calmly and factually. Google rewards engagement.
- Goal: a steady trickle beats a one-time burst. Even **5–10 real reviews** puts you ahead of most local competitors who never ask.

## F. Keep it active (10 min/week)

- Post a **Google Business "Update"** weekly — your before/after of the week, an offer, or a tip. (The content calendar already produces these.)
- Keep services/hours current. Activity signals Google the business is live and real.

# PART 2 — House-watch checklist distribution plan

**The play:** the checklist is the wedge — a genuinely useful free thing that gets us in front of absentee owners and the locals who know them, and opens the door to the email sequence and the house-watch service. We seed it where OBX owners and locals already gather. **Lead with value, not a sales pitch** — these communities punish ads and reward helpfulness.

## Where to post (ranked)

1. **Local OBX Facebook groups** — the big one. Examples to target (confirm exact group names/rules): "Outer Banks Locals," OBX community/news groups, town-specific groups (KDH, Nags Head, Corolla), and OBX second-home / vacation-rental-owner groups. **Read each group's rules first** — some ban promotion outright; in those, post as a helpful resource with no hard sell, or skip.
2. **Nextdoor** — neighborhood-trusted; ideal for the homeowner/house-watch angle. Post in your own and nearby neighborhoods.
3. **Facebook Marketplace** — list the *services* (pressure washing, house-watch, junk removal) with the checklist offered as a free add-on to anyone who messages. Marketplace is search-driven and great for washing/junk leads.
4. **Your own OBX Lot Solutions Facebook Page** — post it there too; pin it.

## Rules of the road (so we don't get banned or ignored)

- **Check group rules before every post.** When promotion is allowed only on certain days (many groups have "Self-Promo Sunday"), use them.
- **Lead with the free value.** The checklist is the hook; the service is the soft follow-up.
- **Don't spam.** Space posts out; don't blanket ten groups in one hour with identical text — vary it (versions below).
- **Engage the comments.** Reply to every question; that's where the leads actually come from. Deliver the checklist via DM or a link, and add commenters to the email sequence (with consent) on approval.
- **Native first.** Where allowed, post the checklist image/PDF directly rather than only a link — platforms suppress outbound links.
