# 2-Week Content Calendar — OBX Lot Solutions + Jamieson Automation

*The first two weeks of the proof machine. OBX-weighted (~70/30), free channels only (community groups, Google Business Profile, email) per the Channel Plan — no ad spend required. Every post ladders to a content pillar and most point back to the **Home-Watch Checklist** lead magnet, the wedge offer.*

**Standing rule: nothing posts or sends without Ray's approval. These are drafts staged for review.**

---

## How to read this

- **Brand:** 🟢 OBX (green `#8BC34A` / navy `#1B2A4E`, dependable-neighbor voice) · 🔵 Jamieson (navy `#002052` / blue `#0099E5`, expert-pro voice).
- **Pillar:** Proof · Education · Offer · Reviews/Social proof.
- **Channel:** GBP = Google Business Profile post · FB/ND = local Facebook groups + Nextdoor · Email = list nurture.
- **Asset needed:** what Marketing has to produce before it can go (a 📸 = needs a real job photo from Ray).
- Cadence: ~1 OBX post most days, Jamieson ~2x/week, light weekends. Reviews/referral asks happen continuously off every completed job, not just on calendar days.

## Recurring engines (run continuously, not on a single day)

- **Review & referral asks** — every completed job, every week. Highest close-rate channel there is.
- **Photo capture** — every job is content. Before/after, same angle, into the shared folder. One job → GBP post + community post + website gallery + review request + future ad creative.
- **PM outreach support** — as Ray works the property-manager list, Marketing keeps the leave-behind, the checklist, and the one-page portfolio sheet current so every touch looks professional.
