## 🚀 Launch order-of-operations (the one sequence to follow)

> Full detail lives in **Launch-Day Playbook.md** — this is the map.

**STEP 1 — Google Business Profile (do first).** Ray creates + verifies it (Google requires the owner). Categories, description, photos, review link — all drafted in the playbook. *Why first: it's the #1 free local-search asset and reviews take time to accumulate.*

**STEP 2 — Distribute the checklist.** Post the house-watch checklist to FB groups / Nextdoor / Marketplace using the ready-to-paste copy (Versions A–D in the playbook). Pin it on the Page. *This is the wedge — it feeds everything else.*

**STEP 3 — Run Week-1 posts.** Follow the first-week schedule, using the captions + graphics already built. Repeat with Week 2.

**STEP 4 — Turn on the email nurture.** Anyone who downloads the checklist enters the 4-email House-Watch sequence. Jamieson PM outreach runs in parallel off the shared PM list.

**Always-on from day one:** ask for a Google review on every job · capture before/after photos (Shot Guide) · reply to every comment/DM · work the PM call list.
